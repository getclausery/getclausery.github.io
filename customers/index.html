Customers & use cases

Who uses Clausery

People and small businesses who send the same kinds of Word documents again and again, and do not want to upload client details to a document server.

Every scenario below links to a real page with templates and guidance — no invented quotes.

Freelancers & solo creators

Designers, writers, developers, photographers, videographers, coaches and tutors who send invoices and contracts themselves.

Used for

  • Invoice by the hour, day or project with line-item calculations and tax
  • Quote a fixed scope, then turn it into an invoice when accepted
  • Send a simple freelance contract or statement of work before starting work
  • Remember business details, bank details and the next invoice number

Popular templates: Invoice · Price quote · Independent contractor agreement · Freelance writing contract, more

Common questions

Do I need an account for any of this?

No. Every template in the free library is free with no sign-up. Start from any “Fill it in” button and download a finished Word document. Pro ($19/mo after a 14-day no-card trial) removes the “Made with Clausery” line and unlocks unlimited own templates, calculations, encryption and bulk-from-spreadsheet.

Which plans include which features?

See the pricing page for the full comparison. Free: all 87 library templates, up to three of your own, unlimited documents. Pro: unlimited own templates, calculations, encrypted workspace, intake forms, unlimited bulk-from-spreadsheet. Team: five seats, onboarding, shared template packs. Enterprise: commercial license, self-hosting, custom branding.

Can my whole team share templates?

Yes. Pro works per user for individuals; Team covers five named users with shared template packs and an onboarding call. Enterprise adds self-hosting and a commercial license for white-label or intranet deployments.

What is the money-back guarantee?

If Clausery is not right for you, email within 30 days of your first payment for a full refund. No forms, and you do not need to give a reason.
