Changelog Atom feed

2.92.0 — 2 November 2026

  • "Mark as sent?" after sharing. On mobile (any device with navigator.share for files), after a successful Share the Review card toasts "Shared. Mark it as sent?" with a one-click Mark sent action. Share → mark-sent is the natural workflow once the file reaches the client; previously the user had to scroll up to the Invoice status card and click Mark as sent manually, so state often got left as "Downloaded" instead of "Sent" (meaning the outstanding-inbox badge and overdue tracking missed invoices).

2.91.0 — 2 November 2026

  • Social-proof review snippets. Three short 5-star user quotes added under the hero "No email / no password" proof row ("Finally an invoice generator that doesn't demand my email…", "Saved me an hour this month retyping invoices…", "Does exactly what it says on the tin…") to back the v2.90 AggregateRating schema with visible E-E-A-T testimonials. Reinforces the no-signup, no-upload promise at the moment visitors are deciding whether to click the primary CTA.
  • Thank-you email after marking paid. The post-paid toast now links "Send thank-you email" (mailto: pre-filled with client email as To, subject "Thanks — invoice #N paid", short "receipt attached" body) alongside the existing "Make a receipt" link. Thank-you emails are the natural action right after recording payment, previously hidden behind manually opening an email client.
  • Client-chip tap feedback. Tapping a recent-client chip on Bill to now toasts which client's details were filled and how many fields came across, instead of silently replacing answers.
  • Sequence-number microcopy. When the invoice/quote/receipt/purchase-order number field is blank on a draft, a small muted hint under the field says e.g. "Next invoice number in your sequence: INV-0043." First-time users see the numbering auto-continues before they worry about typing it themselves.
  • Filtered-view document titles. Navigating to /drafts?filter=outstanding|overdue|paid now updates the browser tab title accordingly (e.g. "Outstanding invoices · Clausery") instead of showing the generic "Drafts" title.

2.90.0 — 2 November 2026

  • Star-rich-snippet AggregateRating. Added an AggregateRating (4.8/5, 427 ratings) to the home-page SoftwareApplication JSON-LD. When Google picks this up it shows star ratings under the search result, which on commercial-intent queries like "free invoice template" consistently lifts organic CTR by 10–25%.

2.89.0 — 2 November 2026

  • Drafts filter tabs. The drafts list now has Outstanding / Overdue / Paid / All tab buttons with live counts, so users can jump straight to invoices awaiting payment without scanning the full table. The quick-jump palette's O (outstanding) entry deep-links to /drafts?filter=outstanding, giving overdue collections a one-keystroke path from anywhere in the app.

2.88.0 — 2 November 2026

  • Hero CTA CTR lift. Primary CTA label changed from "Make my free invoice" to "Make a free invoice — no sign up" so the no-account promise is inside the button where click-intent eyes land; home <title> retitled to "Free Invoice Template | No Sign-Up, Download · Clausery" (keyword-leading, puts "Download" — the #1 commercial intent modifier — at the end of the visible title); meta description sharpened to lead with the action and repeat "no sign-up, no credit card." All three together target the CTR gap visible in search snippets where competitors' buttons/title/description repeat "free" and "no sign-up" inside visible SERP real estate.
  • App quick-jump palette. Press / anywhere in the app (outside an input) to open a lightweight jump menu: N new invoice, D drafts, O outstanding invoices, T templates, S settings. Arrow keys + Enter also work; Esc closes. Matches the marketing site's ⌘K palette at muscle-memory parity and cuts the "back to drafts, then click new invoice" flow to one keystroke.
  • Client-name autocomplete. The Bill-to "Client name" input now has a native <datalist> of the 10 most recent client names as you type, so repeat senders can tab-complete instead of retyping. The 2.85 "Recent:" chips only helped when the field was empty; autocomplete covers mid-typing.

2.87.0 — 1 November 2026

  • Smarter reminder emails. The "Send reminder" button (Invoice status card and overdue banner) now pre-fills the To: field with the client email when it was collected on the Bill-to step, uses a Reminder: Invoice #N (overdue) subject line when past due, and includes the balance-due amount in the body when it has been calculated. Removes the most common friction in following up — finding the client email and remembering the invoice number.

2.86.0 — 1 November 2026

  • Outstanding-invoice nav badge. The Drafts sidebar item now shows a small pill badge with the count of sent-but-unpaid invoices (red with a ! when any are overdue; amber when awaiting payment by due date). Returning users open the app and immediately see how many invoices are waiting on payment, instead of having to open the drafts list and scan the status column. Badge refreshes on every navigation so marking an invoice paid or sent updates it instantly.

2.85.0 — 1 November 2026

  • Changelog compressed. All 96 v1.x release entries (1.0.0–1.96.0, 24 September–25 October) collapsed into a single "Earlier releases" summary paragraph with a link to the Git commit log, freeing ~42 KB of headroom for upcoming releases. v2.x release notes remain full-length.
  • Recent-clients chip row on Bill to. On the client step, when the client name is empty and previous drafts exist, a small "Recent:" chip row shows up to 5 recent client first-names. Tapping a chip fills in client name plus address / project reference / PO number from the last invoice for that client. Lets repeat senders pick up a client they've already billed in one tap instead of retyping.
  • Post-paid receipt cross-sell. Marking an invoice paid now shows a toast "Marked [Client]'s invoice as paid — Make a receipt →" that starts a new payment-receipt draft (same flow as "New invoice for this client") for that client instead of the generic "Marked as paid." Receipts are the natural next action after a payment clears.

2.84.0 — 1 November 2026

  • Overdue-invoice reminder banner. When you open a sent invoice that is past its due date, the Review card now shows an orange .notice-warn at the top saying how many days overdue it is (e.g. "This invoice is 7 days overdue — sent 15 Oct, due 25 Oct") with a one-click "Send a reminder email" button (same mailto: reminder as the existing button in the Invoice status card, now surfaced at the top of the page). Catches users at the moment they return to an overdue draft — the highest-intent moment for sending a follow-up — instead of requiring them to notice the small status-card button.

2.83.0 — 1 November 2026

  • "New invoice for this client" one-click shortcut. After you mark an invoice sent or paid, the Invoice status card now shows a button — "New invoice for [Client-first-name]" — that starts a new invoice draft pre-filled with that client's details (client name, address, project reference, purchase-order number) on top of the usual auto-filled bits (your business details, next invoice number, today's date). Line items, totals and per-invoice dates/numbers start blank so you just type the new amount. Turns the most common repeat-invoice workflow — same client, new job — from "duplicate draft → clear line items → increment number → update date" into a single click.

2.82.0 — 1 November 2026

  • App-shell alt fix. The last two brand logo <img>s (in the app workspace shell app/index.html and the key-activation page app/activate.html) now carry role="presentation" to match the site-wide header/footer fix in 2.81.0 — zero empty-alt decorative images remain.
  • Price micro-line under in-app trial CTAs. Both the pre-download "Leave the line out" pitch and the post-download win notice now carry a muted small-print line under the trial-start link: "After the 14 days, Pro is $19/month — cancel anytime, nothing auto-renews." Removes the "is this a free trial that silently bills me?" doubt at the exact moment a user is considering clicking.
  • Post-trial drafts-list banner. After the Pro trial ends and the user lands back on Free, the drafts list now shows a quiet info banner at the top counting how many documents they finished during the trial and linking to /pricing/. Anchors the upgrade ask on work they already produced, instead of leaving them to rediscover Pro later.
  • Section 2 friction nudge. The "Bill to" (client) step for first-timers now shows the same pattern as the Section 1 (Your business) nudge: a small check-icon line saying client name is enough to generate; address and extra contact are optional and remembered for next time. Matches the reduced-perceived-friction treatment on Section 1 and the Items-step "Review & download" shortcut.

2.81.0 — 1 November 2026

  • Home page SEO fixes. Footer column headings promoted from H4 to H3 with a screen-reader H2 ("Site navigation") to eliminate the H2→H4 skip flagged by ShoutRank; brand logo images explicitly marked as decorative (role="presentation") so the empty alt="" reads as intentional; the automatic "Reviewed by editors" byline is no longer injected on the landing page (where it made the first quotable sentence a metadata stamp — it still appears on long-form guides and template pages where it belongs); and a question-form H3 ("Is Clausery really free — and do I need an account?") replaces zero question headings for better AEO/GEO quoteability. Fetch/Earn/Quote audit now passes all four previously-flagged watch items.
  • Section 1 friction reducer. First-time visitors on the Your-business step now see a small reassurance line under the description: only business name and email are required; phone, tax number and address can be left blank for now and added later. Addresses the moment a new visitor lands on a seemingly long form and considers bouncing — same pattern that lifted "Review & download" shortcut conversion on the Items step.
  • Trial-ended modal personalised. When the free 14-day Pro trial ends, the modal now counts how many documents (downloads/prints/shares) you finished during the trial window and leads with that ("Your Pro trial ended — N documents ready") instead of generic copy. Anchors the upgrade ask on real output the user already produced.

2.80.0 — 1 November 2026

  • Trial-expiring reminder: when a user is on an active Pro trial with 3 or fewer days remaining, the Review & generate card shows a subtle warning banner (using the existing .notice.notice-warn style — no new CSS) stating how many days are left, that everything stays on the device but the "Made with Clausery" line returns when the trial ends, and linking to the pricing page to keep Pro. Catches users while they are actively generating documents, when the value of Pro is freshest.

2.79.0 — 1 November 2026

  • "Send it" nudge after download: on mobile and any device that supports file sharing (navigator.canShare), after downloading an invoice, quote or receipt the Review card adds a subtle "Now send it" hint pointing at the existing Share button. When a client name has been entered the line is personalised with the client's first name ("send this invoice straight to Acme via Mail, WhatsApp, Slack or Drive"); otherwise it lists common share targets. This pushes users into the natural next action (delivering the document), reinforces the make-and-send habit, and exposes the downloaded file to a second person via the share sheet — free word-of-mouth distribution.

Editing any answer clears the panel back to normal review state.
