Frequently asked questions

Which browsers are supported?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile. License keys are verified with WebCrypto Ed25519 (Chrome/Edge 137+, Firefox 129+, Safari 17+). The encrypted workspace uses PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM, available in all current browsers.

Does it work with Google Docs or LibreOffice?

Yes, as long as you export or save as .docx. Tags are plain text, so any editor can write them.

My formatting looks different in the preview.

The on-screen preview is an approximation rendered in the browser. The downloaded .docx is assembled from your original file and opens in Word with your exact formatting. Use Print / Save as PDF for a quick PDF, or open the .docx in Word for a print-perfect one.

Is there a limit on template size or number of drafts?

No fixed limit. Browsers typically allow hundreds of megabytes of local storage; a template is usually well under a megabyte and a draft a few kilobytes.

Can several people work on one draft?

Not at the same time; there is no server to coordinate. Hand over a draft by exporting and importing its answers file, share templates as a template pack, and send finished documents with the Share button where your device supports it.

Can I make many documents at once from a spreadsheet?

Yes. On the Review & generate step, choose From a spreadsheet, download the spreadsheet template (one column per question), add a row per document in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers, save it as CSV and choose it. Each row fills in or replaces the draft's answers (empty cells keep them) and you get one .docx per row in a .zip. The Free plan makes up to 5 documents per spreadsheet; Pro has no limit. The spreadsheet never leaves your browser.

Is there a record of what was generated and when?

Yes. Every draft keeps a history of each document made from it: downloaded, printed, shared or made from a spreadsheet, with the time, the file name and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact answers used. You can restore any earlier set of answers. The history is stored with the draft in your browser and in backups; it is a working record for you, not a tamper-proof legal audit log.

Does Clausery do e-signatures?

No. A legally robust e-signature needs a service that holds the document and records each signer, which Clausery deliberately does not do. Download the Word file or print to PDF and send it for signature with the service you already use, such as DocuSign, Adobe Acrobat Sign or Dropbox Sign.

Can it send documents to Slack, email or accounting software?

Not automatically: there are no webhooks or integrations, because nothing leaves your browser. Where your device supports it (most phones, and Chrome, Edge and Safari on many desktops) the Share… button hands the finished document to Mail, Slack, Teams, Drive and other apps in one step.

Are the templates valid where I live?

The library templates are general samples, written mainly for the US, Canada and the UK, with notes where the law commonly differs, such as non-compete limits or tax invoice rules. Each page shows when it was last updated, and the guides cite their sources. Laws differ by country, state and city, so have a lawyer review anything important for your situation.

Is there an official version?

Yes: getclausery.github.io. The source code is public on GitHub, but copies hosted elsewhere are not maintained by us. Releases are listed in the changelog.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The app is responsive and installable. Drafting long documents is more comfortable on a larger screen.

What if Clausery disappears?

Your copy keeps working: it is cached in your browser and can be self-hosted from the repository. Your templates are your .docx files; your data exports are plain JSON.

How do I get a license key?

See Pricing. Keys are delivered by email and entered once per device.
