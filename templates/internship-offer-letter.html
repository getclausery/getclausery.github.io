This is the complete wording. [Labels] are filled in from your answers, and highlighted text only appears when it applies; hover over it to see when.

INTERNSHIP OFFER

[Offer date]

Dear [Candidate first name],

We are pleased to offer you an internship as [Internship title] in our [Team name] team at [Company name], starting on [Start date] and ending on [End date].

You will be paid [Pay rate] [Pay basis].This is an unpaid internship, with a stipend of [Stipend amount] towards expenses.

You will work [Hours per week] hours per week, remotely, at our [Office location] office. Your supervisor will be [Supervisor name].

During the internship you will work on [Learning objectives].

We will provide any documentation your school requires for academic credit.

Please confirm your acceptance by [Response deadline].

Welcome aboard,

[Signatory name]

[Signatory title], [Company name]

Can internships be unpaid?

In many places unpaid internships are only lawful in narrow circumstances. Check your local rules before offering one.

Yes. One question switches between remote and a named office.
