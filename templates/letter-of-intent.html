Questions

Is a letter of intent binding?

Usually only in part. This letter says the price and deal terms are not binding until a definitive agreement is signed, while exclusivity, confidentiality and costs are. Courts can still find a deal was agreed if the parties act as if it was, so keep the wording and your conduct consistent.

What is the difference between a letter of intent and an MOU?

Both record intentions before a final agreement. A letter of intent is usually a one-sided offer in letter form, common for buying a business or property; an MOU is usually a joint statement between two partners.
