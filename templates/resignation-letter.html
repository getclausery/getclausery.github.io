LETTER OF RESIGNATION

[Letter date]

Dear [Manager name],

Please accept this letter as formal notice of my resignation from my position as [Job title] at [Company name]. My last working day will be [Last day], in line with my notice period of [Notice period].

I have decided to [Resignation reason].

I will do everything I can to ensure a smooth handover, including [Handover plan].

Thank you for the opportunities I have had during my time here. I have particularly valued [Appreciation].

Yours sincerely,

[Employee name]

Questions

Do I have to give a reason?

No. The reason is optional and many people leave it out.

How much notice should I give?

Check your contract; two to four weeks is common, and longer for senior roles.

Is it really free?

Yes. The download is free, and filling it in with the Clausery app is free too, with unlimited documents. Library templates never count towards a plan limit, and no account or card is needed.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Clausery runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and the finished document are created and stored on your own device.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are general samples. Laws differ between countries and states, so have the wording reviewed for your situation before you rely on it.
