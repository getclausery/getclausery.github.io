Questions

What should an expense reimbursement form include?

Who is claiming, the period, each expense with its date, purpose, category and amount, the total, receipts, and a manager's approval.

What mileage rate should I use?

Many US businesses use the IRS standard mileage rate, which changes each year; other countries publish their own rates. Use your company's policy if it has one.

What if I lost a receipt?

Explain what the expense was and why the receipt is missing. Many policies accept a written explanation for small amounts.
