Questions

What is a credit note?

A document that reduces the amount a customer owes on an invoice you have already issued, for example after a return, an overcharge or an agreed discount.

Can I just edit the original invoice instead?

It is better not to. Issuing a credit note keeps your invoice numbers and records consistent, which accountants and tax authorities expect.

Does a credit note need tax on it?

If the original invoice charged sales tax or VAT, the credit note should reverse the tax on the credited amount too.
