What is in the offer letter

  • Role, manager and location (remote or office)
  • Start date
  • Base salary
  • Optional bonus target
  • Optional equity grant with vesting
  • Benefits list and paid time off
  • Conditions such as a background check
  • Acceptance block

Read the full template

This is the complete wording. [Labels] are filled in from your answers, and highlighted text only appears when it applies; hover over it to see when.

OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT

[Offer date]

Dear [Candidate first name],

We are delighted to offer you the position of [Job title] at [Company name] (the "Company"), reporting to [Manager name], [Manager title]. This is a remote position.You will be based at our [Office location] office.

Start date

Your anticipated start date is [Start date], subject to the conditions below.

Compensation

Your [Pay basis] salary will be [Salary], paid in accordance with the Company's standard payroll schedule and subject to applicable withholdings.

You will be eligible for a discretionary annual bonus with a target of [Bonus target] of your base salary, based on Company and individual performance.

Subject to approval by the board of directors, you will be granted [Equity amount] under the Company's equity incentive plan, vesting over [Vesting years] years with a one-year cliff.

Benefits

You will be eligible for the following benefits, subject to the terms of each plan:

Repeated for each benefit

  • [Item]

You will accrue [Pto days] days of paid time off per year.

Conditions

This offer is contingent on satisfactory completion of a background check and proof of your eligibility to work in [Work country]. Employment is [Employment terms].

Please confirm your acceptance by signing below and returning this letter by [Response deadline].

We look forward to working with you.

Sincerely,

[Signatory name]

[Signatory title], [Company name]

ACCEPTED:

____________________________

[Candidate full name]

Date: ______________

Questions

Is this an employment contract?

No. It is an offer letter. Employment terms, at-will language and required notices depend on your country and state.

Can I reuse it for every hire?

Yes. Answer the questions for each candidate and download a finished letter in seconds; every draft is kept for later.

Is it really free?

Yes. The download is free, and filling it in with the Clausery app is free too, with unlimited documents. Library templates never count towards a plan limit, and no account or card is needed.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Clausery runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and the finished document are created and stored on your own device.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are general samples. Laws differ between countries and states, so have the wording reviewed for your situation before you rely on it.
