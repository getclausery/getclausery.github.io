Questions

What should meeting minutes include?

The date, time and place, who attended, each item discussed and what was decided, the actions agreed with who will do them and by when, and when the meeting ended. Minutes record decisions, not every word said.

Are minutes legally required?

Often. Companies generally must keep minutes of board and shareholder meetings, and many nonprofits and clubs must keep them under their bylaws or constitution. Even where they are optional, they are the record of what was agreed.

Who approves the minutes?

Usually the same group at its next meeting, which agrees them as a correct record; the chair then signs them. Corrections are made before they are approved.
