Business agreements

Business

Independent contractor agreement

A contractor agreement covering services, hourly or fixed fees, invoicing, independent status, ownership of work product, confidentiality and termination.

Business

Statement of work

A statement of work with a list of deliverables and due dates, a timeline, fixed-price or time-and-materials fees, assumptions and acceptance.

Business

Consulting agreement

A consulting agreement with a monthly retainer or a day rate, a list of deliverables with due dates, confidentiality, ownership of materials and a notice period.

Business

Service agreement

A general service agreement with a priced list of services, payment terms, optional deposit and late fee, a fixed or auto-renewing term, standard of work and a liability cap.

Business

Subcontractor agreement

A subcontractor agreement for agencies, consultants and freelancers who pass part of a client project to another contractor: deliverables, fixed or hourly fee, optional pay-when-paid, white-label and non-solicitation terms, and ownership that passes to you.

Business

Memorandum of understanding (MOU)

A memorandum of understanding for two organisations working together: the purpose, what each side will do, how costs are shared, the term, optional confidentiality, and a clear choice of whether it is legally binding.

Business

Letter of intent (business purchase)

A letter of intent to buy a business or its assets: the price and how it is paid, an optional earn-out, due diligence, closing conditions, optional exclusivity, confidentiality and costs, with everything except those protections left non-binding.

Business

Partnership agreement

A general partnership agreement for two or more partners: each partner's contribution and share, how profits and losses are split, drawings and salaries, day-to-day and major decisions, a spending limit, the bank account, leaving and buyout, dissolution and disputes.

Business

Sales commission agreement

A sales commission agreement for an employee or independent sales representative: the products and territory, the commission rate with an optional higher rate above a threshold, when commission is earned, chargebacks, monthly payment with a statement, an optional draw, expenses, and commission on orders after the agreement ends.

Freelance contracts

Freelance

Web design contract

A web design contract that lists every page, sets the launch date and content deadline, limits revision rounds, and covers the deposit, optional monthly maintenance and who owns the finished site.

Freelance

Graphic design contract

A graphic design contract for logos, brand identities and print or digital work, with deliverables and file formats, concept and revision limits, a flat or hourly fee, and a choice between transferring copyright or licensing the design.

Freelance

Photography contract

A photography contract for events, portraits and commercial shoots, covering the date, location and hours, a booking retainer, delivery of edited images, optional prints and travel fees, cancellation, and personal or commercial usage rights.

Freelance

Social media management contract

A social media management contract listing each platform and posting frequency, with optional community management, paid ads and reporting, an approval process, a monthly fee, a minimum term and clear ownership of accounts and content.

Freelance

Freelance writing contract

A freelance writing contract with a list of assignments, each with its word count, due date and fee, plus revisions, originality and optional AI-use disclosure, payment and kill fee, rights and byline.

Freelance

Video production contract

A video production contract listing each video with its length and format, the shoot and delivery dates, edit rounds, deposit and expenses, who owns the final videos and the raw footage, and cancellation terms.

Freelance

Virtual assistant agreement

A virtual assistant agreement with a task list, a monthly retainer of hours or an hourly rate, availability and response times, secure handling of logins, optional data handling terms, and notice to end.

Freelance

Event planning contract

An event planning contract covering the event, a list of services, optional day-of coordination, a flat or percentage fee with a retainer, how vendors are booked and paid, cancellation, postponement and events beyond anyone's control.

Freelance

Personal training agreement

A personal training agreement for session packages or pay-per-session, with a late cancellation rule, a health declaration, optional medical clearance, a plain statement of exercise risk and refund terms.

Freelance

Tutoring agreement

A tutoring agreement between a tutor and a parent covering subjects and goals, online or in-person sessions, hourly fees, cancellation, optional progress reports, safeguarding and academic honesty.

Freelance

Retainer agreement

A monthly retainer agreement for freelancers, consultants and agencies: the services included, a monthly fee with optional included hours, rollover and extra-hours rate, response times, late payment terms, a minimum term and notice to end.

Freelance

Photo and model release

A model release for photographs, video and audio: who may use the images, for commercial use or only the purposes you list, for a set number of years or without limit, whether the model's name may be used, any restrictions, payment, and a parent or guardian consent for a model under 18.

Loans and getting paid

Finance

Payment demand letter

A firm but polite letter asking a customer to pay an overdue balance by a deadline, with optional invoice number, interest and next steps.

Finance

Promissory note

A promissory note for a personal or business loan with the principal, optional interest, lump-sum or instalment repayment, prepayment, default after a grace period and an optional witness.

Finance

Payment reminder letter

A payment reminder letter for an overdue invoice, from a friendly first reminder to a final notice before further action, with optional part payment, late fee and the date you will act.

Finance

Loan agreement

A loan agreement for a family, friend or business loan: the amount and purpose, optional interest capped at the legal maximum, one repayment or instalments, early repayment, an optional late fee, collateral and guarantor, default and governing law.

Landlords and tenants

Real estate

Notice to vacate (tenant)

A tenant's written notice to the landlord that they are moving out: the move-out date, whether the lease is ending or how many days' notice is given, optional co-tenants, a request for a move-out inspection, returning keys, and where to send the security deposit.

Real estate

Rent increase letter

A landlord's written notice of a rent increase: the current and new monthly rent, the date it starts, the notice given for a month-to-month tenancy or a lease that is ending, an optional reason, an optional renewal offer and a date to reply by.

Real estate

Security deposit return letter

A landlord's itemized statement of what happens to a tenant's security deposit after they move out: the deposit and any interest, a list of deductions with amounts, the total deducted, the amount refunded and how it is paid, and optional receipts.

Real estate

Roommate agreement

An agreement between people who share a rented home: each roommate's share of the rent, who pays the landlord, the deposit, utilities and other shared costs, quiet time, guests, cleaning, pets and smoking, notice before moving out, and house meetings.

NDAs, letters and sales

Legal

Mutual NDA

A two-way non-disclosure agreement for when both sides will share confidential information: partnership talks, M&A conversations, joint ventures, vendor evaluations.

Legal

Engagement letter

An engagement letter that sets out scope, the team, fees and billing for a new matter. It supports hourly or flat fees, an optional retainer, and a list of attorneys with their rates.

Legal

One-way NDA

A unilateral non-disclosure agreement for when only one side shares confidential information: pitching an idea, hiring a contractor, showing a prototype or sharing financials with a potential buyer.

Legal

Cease and desist letter

A cease and desist letter that describes the conduct, states the basis of the complaint, lists exactly what the recipient must do, sets a deadline and reserves your rights.

Legal

Bill of sale

A general bill of sale for a car, boat, equipment, furniture or any other item: the buyer and seller, what was sold, the price, full payment or a deposit and balance, ownership, an as-is or warranty choice, and optional vehicle, witness and notary sections.

Legal

General release

A release agreement that settles a dispute: the payment or other value given, a general release of all claims or only those about the dispute, an optional mutual release, an optional waiver of unknown claims, no admission of liability, withdrawal of any pending case, and optional confidentiality.

HR letters

HR

Offer letter

A job offer letter with remote or office variants, salary, optional bonus and equity, a benefits list and offer conditions.

HR

Employment verification letter

A letter confirming that someone works, or used to work, for your company, with their job title, dates and employment type, and optionally their salary.

HR

Termination letter

A clear, respectful notice of termination covering the end date, notice or pay in lieu, final pay, optional severance, return of company property and who to contact.

HR

Reference letter

A reference letter for a former employee or colleague with their role, dates, responsibilities, a list of strengths and an optional recommendation for a specific role.

HR

Salary increase letter

A short letter confirming a pay rise: the old and new salary, the effective date, the first payslip that shows it, and an optional reason and new job title.

HR

Internship offer letter

An internship offer covering dates, paid or unpaid status, an optional stipend, weekly hours, remote or office location, supervisor, learning objectives and academic credit.

HR

Resignation letter

A professional resignation letter with your last working day, notice period, an optional reason, an offer to help with the handover and an optional thank-you.
