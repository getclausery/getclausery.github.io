Common questions

Are the templates really free?

Yes. Every template on this page is free to fill in online and download as an editable Word .docx. No account, no credit card and no watermark on the document. The Free plan lets you make unlimited documents; paid Pro and Team plans add team vaults, branding and saved drafts.

Do I need to sign up or give an email?

No. You can open any template, answer the questions and download a finished .docx without creating an account or giving an email address. Nothing you type is uploaded — the document is assembled in your browser.

Can I edit the downloaded Word file?

Yes. Downloads are standard .docx files that open in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, Apple Pages and any other editor that reads Word. You can keep editing after download; the {curly-brace} placeholders become plain text once you download.
