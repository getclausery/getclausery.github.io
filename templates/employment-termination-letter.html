  • Employee, role and termination date
  • Optional reason
  • Notice period, garden leave, or pay in lieu of notice
  • Final pay and accrued time off
  • Optional severance
  • Return of company property

Read the full template

This is the complete wording. [Labels] are filled in from your answers, and highlighted text only appears when it applies; hover over it to see when.

NOTICE OF TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

[Letter date]

[Employee name]

[Employee address]

Dear [Employee first name],

This letter confirms that your employment with [Company name] as [Job title] will end on [Termination date].

The reason for this decision is [Termination reason].

You will receive pay in lieu of notice for [Notice period].Your notice period is [Notice period], and you will not be required to work during it.

Your final pay, including any accrued but unused paid time off, will be paid on [Final pay date]. In addition, you will receive a severance payment of [Severance amount], subject to the terms set out in a separate agreement.

Please return all company property, including [Property to return], by [Return by date].

Information about your benefits after your employment ends, including any continuation options, will be sent to you separately.

If you have questions, please contact [Hr contact name] at [Hr contact email].

Sincerely,

[Signatory name]

[Signatory title], [Company name]

Questions

Do I have to give a reason?

It depends on your country, state and contract. The reason line is optional; take advice before terminating anyone.

Is this enough to terminate someone lawfully?

No letter alone makes a termination lawful. Notice rules, consultation, final pay deadlines and protected characteristics all vary by jurisdiction.
