What is in the personal training agreement

  • Session package with expiry, or price per session
  • Session length and location
  • Late cancellation rule
  • Health declaration and optional doctor's clearance
  • Exercise risk, without excluding liability the law does not allow
  • No guaranteed results
  • Refund or transfer of unused sessions

Read the full template

PERSONAL TRAINING AGREEMENT

This Personal Training Agreement is made on [Effective date] between [Trainer name] (the "Trainer") and [Client name] (the "Client").

1. Sessions

The Client buys a package of [Session count] sessions for [Package price], to be used within [Package weeks] weeks.Sessions cost [Session price] each. Each session lasts [Session length] minutes and takes place at [Training location].

2. Scheduling and cancellation

Sessions cancelled with less than [Cancellation hours] hours' notice count as used. If the Trainer cancels, the session will be rescheduled at no charge.

3. Health

The Client confirms that they know of no medical reason why they should not exercise, and will tell the Trainer about any injury, condition or medication that could affect training. Before the first session the Client will provide written clearance from a doctor.

4. Risk

The Client understands that exercise carries some risk of injury and agrees to follow the Trainer's instructions and stop if they feel unwell. Nothing in this Agreement limits the Trainer's liability for negligence where the law does not allow it to be limited.

5. Results

The Trainer will use reasonable skill and care but cannot guarantee any particular result.

6. Payment

Sessions and packages are paid in advance. If the Client cannot continue for medical reasons, unused sessions will be refunded.Packages are not refundable, but unused sessions can be transferred to another person with the Trainer's agreement.

7. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law].

[Trainer name]: ____________________________

[Client name]: ____________________________

Questions

Can I make clients waive all liability?

No. Most legal systems do not allow you to exclude liability for your own negligence causing injury, and trying to can undermine the whole clause. The template states the risks plainly instead.

Should I ask for medical clearance?

For clients with known conditions, injuries or who are returning to exercise, asking for a doctor's clearance is sensible. One question adds it.

Is it really free?

Yes. The download is free, and filling it in with the Clausery app is free too, with unlimited documents. Library templates never count towards a plan limit, and no account or card is needed.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Clausery runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and the finished document are created and stored on your own device.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are general samples. Laws differ between countries and states, so have the wording reviewed for your situation before you rely on it.
