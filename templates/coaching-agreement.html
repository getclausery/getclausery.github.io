COACHING AGREEMENT

This Coaching Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Coach name] (the "Coach") and [Client name] (the "Client").

1. The program

The Coach will provide [Program name]: [Session count] groupone-to-one sessions of [Session length], held [Session format], starting on [Start date]. The focus is [Coaching focus]. Between sessions, the Client may message the Coach by [Messaging channel], and the Coach replies within [Reply days] business days.

2. Fees

The fee for the program is [Package fee], payable [Billing schedule].The fee is [Session fee] per session, payable before each session. Fees are paid by [Method of paying].

3. Rescheduling and cancellation

Either party may reschedule a session with at least [Reschedule notice hours] hours' notice. A session the Client cancels with less notice, or misses, counts as used. Sessions not used within [Sessions expire days] days of the start date expire.

4. What coaching is and is not

Coaching is a partnership to help the Client set and reach their own goals. It is not therapy, counselling, or medical, legal or financial advice, and the Coach will suggest the Client seek a qualified professional where that is needed. The Client is responsible for their own decisions and results, and the Coach does not guarantee any particular outcome.

5. Confidentiality

The Coach keeps what the Client shares confidential, except where the Client agrees, where the law requires disclosure, or where there is a serious risk of harm to the Client or others. Group participants are asked to keep what others share in the group confidential.

6. Ending the agreement

Either party may end this agreement by written notice. If the Client ends it, [Refund terms].The Client pays only for sessions already held. This agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law].

Questions

What should a coaching contract include?

What the client gets (sessions, length, format), the fee and when it is paid, rescheduling and cancellation rules, confidentiality, that coaching is not therapy or professional advice, and how either side can end it.

Should a coaching agreement guarantee results?

No. Results depend on the client's own actions. Promising outcomes invites disputes and, in some places, consumer law problems.

How do refunds work for coaching packages?

Say it in the agreement: for example, a refund for unused sessions, or no refund after a set point. Clear terms avoid arguments later.
