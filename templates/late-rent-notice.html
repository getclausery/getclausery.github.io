What is in the late rent notice

  • Date, tenant and property
  • Rent due and the date it was due
  • Optional partial payment received and the balance
  • Optional late fee under the lease
  • Total due, the date to pay by and how
  • Optional offer of a payment plan
  • Not a notice ending the tenancy
  • Contact details and signature

[Notice date]

[Tenant names]

[Property address]

Re: Late rent notice

Dear [Tenant names],

Our records show that the rent of [Monthly rent] due on [Rent due date] for [Property address] has not been received in full. We received [Amount received], leaving [Amount unpaid] unpaid.

As your lease provides, a late fee of [Late fee amount] now applies. The total now due is [Total due].

Please pay the total due by [Due by date], by [How paid]. If you have already paid, please ignore this notice, and thank you.

If you are having trouble paying, please contact us before that date. We are willing to discuss a payment plan, which would need to be agreed in writing.

This is a reminder under your lease. It is not a notice ending your tenancy. If the rent remains unpaid, we may take further steps that your lease and the law allow.

If you have any questions, please contact [Landlord contact name] at [Landlord phone] or [Landlord email].

Sincerely,

____________________________

[Landlord name]

Questions

Is a late rent notice the same as a pay or quit notice?

No. This is a reminder. A notice to pay rent or quit, the first formal step towards an eviction, has wording, timing and delivery rules set by state law, so use your state's official form or a lawyer for that.

Can I charge a late fee?

Only if the lease allows it, and within any limit set by law. Many states require a grace period or cap late fees, for example at a percentage of the rent. Check the rules where the property is.

When should I send it?

Soon after any grace period in the lease ends. A prompt, polite reminder resolves most late payments, and a written record helps if you need to take further steps.
