VIRTUAL ASSISTANT AGREEMENT

This Virtual Assistant Agreement is made on [Effective date] between [Assistant name] (the "Assistant") and [Client name] (the "Client").

1. Services

The Assistant will provide remote administrative support, including:

Repeated for each task

  • [Task description]

2. Hours and fees

The Client books [Monthly hours] hours per month for [Monthly fee], invoiced in advance. Unused hours do not carry over; extra hours are charged at [Hourly rate] per hour.The Assistant charges [Hourly rate] per hour, invoiced [Invoice frequency]. Invoices are payable within [Payment days] days.

The Assistant is available [Availability] ([Time zone]) and will reply to messages within [Response hours] business hours.

3. Access and confidentiality

The Client will share logins through a password manager where possible. The Assistant will keep the Client's information confidential, use its access only to provide the services, and hand back or remove all access when this Agreement ends.

Where the Assistant handles personal data for the Client, it will do so only on the Client's instructions, keep it secure and delete or return it at the end of the engagement.

4. Tools and expenses

The Client provides any software subscriptions the work requires. Other expenses need the Client's approval in advance.

5. Independent contractor

The Assistant is an independent contractor, may work for other clients, and is responsible for their own taxes and equipment.

6. Term

This Agreement starts on [Start date] and continues until either party gives [Notice days] days' written notice.

7. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law].

[Assistant name]: ____________________________

[Client name]: ____________________________

Questions

Retainer or hourly?

A retainer of monthly hours gives both sides predictable income and cost; hourly suits irregular work. One question switches between them.

What about client data?

If you handle customers' personal data, switch on the data handling clause. Some laws, such as the GDPR, require a written agreement covering this.
