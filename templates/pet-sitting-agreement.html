PET SITTING AGREEMENT

This Pet Sitting Agreement is made on [Agreement date] between [Owner name] (the "Owner") and [Sitter name] (the "Sitter").

1. The pets

Repeated for each pet

  • [Pet name], [Pet type]: [Pet care notes]

Medication: [Medication instructions]

2. Dates and services

The Sitter will care for the pets from [Start date] to [End date] at [Care address]. The Sitter will stay overnight at the Owner's home.The Sitter will visit [Daily visits count] times a day, for about [Visit length] each visit. Care includes feeding, fresh water, walks or exercise, litter or cage cleaning, and [Extra services].

3. Fees

The fee is [Fee amount] [Charged per], for a total of [Total fee]. A deposit of [Deposit amount] is due on booking and is deducted from the total. The balance is due [Balance due terms].

4. Keys and home

The Owner gives the Sitter [Access details]. The Sitter will keep the home locked and secure, use it only to care for the pets, and return all keys at the end of the booking.

5. Emergencies

If a pet is ill or injured, the Sitter will contact the Owner at [Owner phone] and, if the Owner cannot be reached, the emergency contact [Emergency contact name] at [Emergency contact phone]. The Owner authorizes the Sitter to take a pet to [Vet name] or the nearest emergency vet and approve treatment up to [Vet spending limit amount]; the Owner pays all vet costs.

6. Cancellation and responsibility

If the Owner cancels less than [Cancellation days] days before the start date, [Late cancellation terms]. The Owner confirms that the pets are vaccinated as required and have no known history of aggression except as noted above. The Sitter is not responsible for illness, injury or behaviour that is not caused by the Sitter's negligence. This agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law].

Questions

What should a pet sitting contract include?

The pets and their needs, the dates and type of care, the fee and deposit, how the sitter gets into the home, emergency contacts, what the sitter may spend on a vet, and cancellation terms.

Why include a vet authorisation?

So the sitter can get a sick or injured pet treated quickly if the owner cannot be reached, up to an amount the owner has agreed.

Do pet sitters need insurance?

Many carry pet care liability insurance, and some clients ask for it. It is worth mentioning in the agreement if you have it.
