What is in the payment demand letter

  • Debtor details and amount due
  • What the payment was for and when it was due
  • Pay-by date and payment instructions
  • Optional interest
  • Invitation to dispute or agree a payment plan
  • Optional next steps

Read the full template

This is the complete wording. [Labels] are filled in from your answers, and highlighted text only appears when it applies; hover over it to see when.

DEMAND FOR PAYMENT

[Letter date]

[Debtor name]

[Debtor address]

Re: Outstanding balance of [Amount due] (invoice [Invoice number])

Dear [Debtor salutation],

Our records show that [Amount due] for [Goods or services] provided by [Creditor name] was due on [Due date] and remains unpaid.

Please pay the full amount by [Pay by date]. [Payment instructions]

Under the terms agreed between us, interest of [Interest rate] is being added to the overdue balance.

If you have already paid, please disregard this letter and send us the payment details so we can update our records. If you believe the amount is not owed, or you would like to agree a payment plan, please contact [Contact name] at [Contact email] before [Pay by date].

If we do not receive payment or hear from you by that date, we may take further steps to recover the debt, which may include [Next steps].

Sincerely,

[Contact name]

[Creditor name]

Questions

Is this a formal letter before action?

No. Some courts require specific pre-action steps and wording; check your local rules before starting proceedings.

Yes. Download the Word file or print it to PDF and attach it.
