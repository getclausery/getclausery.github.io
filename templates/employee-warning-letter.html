What is in the employee warning letter

  • First or final written warning, in the title and throughout
  • Optional reference to the meeting where it was discussed
  • A dated list of incidents
  • The policy or standard that was not met, and any earlier warning
  • What the employee needs to do, and optional support from the company
  • Review date and how long the warning stays on file
  • What happens if things do not improve
  • Optional right of appeal, and room for the employee's own comments
  • Acknowledgement of receipt, not agreement

The following incidents are the reason for this warning:

Repeated for each incident

  • [Incident date]: [Incident details]

This does not meet the standard expected of you under [Policy reference]. It follows the earlier warning you received on [Previous warning date] about a similar issue.

What needs to change

To bring this matter to a close, you need to: [Improvement steps]

To help you do this, the Company will provide the following support: [Support details]

I will review your progress with you on [Review date].

What happens next

This warning will stay on your personnel file for [Warning months] months. If the required improvement is not made, or if there is any further misconduct during that time, your employment may be terminated.If the required improvement is not made, or the issue happens again, the Company may take further disciplinary action, which could include a final written warning or dismissal.

If you disagree with this warning, you may appeal in writing to [Appeal contact] within [Appeal days] days of receiving this letter, explaining why.

You are welcome to give your own written comments on this letter. They will be kept on your file with it.

Yours sincerely,

[Manager name]

[Manager title], [Company name]

Questions

What should an employee warning letter include?

The facts (what happened and when), the rule or standard it breaks, what the employee needs to do differently and by when, any support you will give, and what happens if nothing changes. Describe behaviour you can show, not opinions about the person.

Does the employee have to sign a warning letter?

No. A signature only confirms they received it, and the letter says so. If they refuse to sign, note the date you gave it to them and who was there.

How many warnings before dismissal?

There is no set number in law in most US states, where employment is usually at will, but many employers use a verbal warning, a written warning and a final written warning. In the UK, following a fair procedure such as the Acas Code of Practice matters if the employee later claims unfair dismissal.
