WEB DESIGN AGREEMENT

This Web Design Agreement is made on [Effective date] between [Designer name] (the "Designer") and [Client name] (the "Client").

1. Project

The Designer will design and build [Project description]. The site will include the following pages:

Repeated for each page

  • [Page name]: [Page notes]

2. Timeline

Work starts on [Start date] and the target launch date is [Launch date]. The Client will provide all text, images and logins the Designer needs by [Content deadline]; later delivery moves the launch date accordingly.

3. Revisions

The fee includes [Revision rounds] rounds of revisions to the approved design. Further changes, or work outside the pages listed above, are charged at [Hourly rate] per hour and agreed in writing first.

4. Fees and payment

The total fee is [Total fee]. A deposit of [Deposit amount] is due on signing and is not refundable once work has started; the balance is due on launch.The fee is due on launch. Invoices are payable within [Payment days] days.

After launch, the Designer will provide hosting support, updates and backups for [Maintenance fee] per month, cancellable by either party on 30 days' notice.

5. Ownership

When the fee has been paid in full, the Client owns the final design and the content created for the site. The Designer keeps ownership of its pre-existing code, tools and templates and grants the Client a perpetual licence to use them as part of the site.

The Designer may show the finished site in its portfolio.

6. Third-party items

Fonts, stock images, plugins and hosting are licensed or paid for by the Client, on the terms of their providers.

7. Termination

Either party may end this Agreement with [Notice days] days' written notice. The Client will pay for work done up to the end of the notice period.

8. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law].

[Designer name]: ____________________________

[Client name]: ____________________________

Questions

Who owns the website after it is paid for?

In this template the client owns the final design and content once the fee is paid in full, while the designer keeps its pre-existing code and tools and licenses them for use in the site. You can change that split in the Word file.

How do I stop endless revisions?

Set the number of revision rounds included in the fee and an hourly rate for anything beyond them. The contract also moves the launch date if the client delivers content late.

Is it really free?

Yes. The template download is free, and the Clausery app is free for up to three templates with unlimited documents. No account or card is needed.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Clausery runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and the finished document are created and stored on your own device.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are general samples. Laws differ between countries and states, so have the wording reviewed for your situation before you rely on it.
