What is in the lease renewal letter

  • Current lease end date
  • New term and dates
  • Rent unchanged or changed
  • Optional other changes
  • Optional month-to-month alternative and its rent
  • Date to reply by, and notice if the tenant is leaving
  • Contact details, signature and the tenant's acceptance

[Letter date]

[Tenant names]

[Property address]

Re: Renewing your lease

Dear [Tenant names],

Your lease for [Property address] ends on [Current lease end date]. We have enjoyed having you as our tenant and would like to offer you a renewal.

The new lease would run for [Renewal length], from [New start date] to [New end date]. The rent would change from [Current rent] to [New rent] a month.The rent would stay at [Current rent] a month. All other terms of your current lease would stay the same, except: [Other changes].

If you would rather not sign for a new fixed term, you can instead continue from month to month after [Current lease end date] at [Month to month rent] a month.

Please let us know by [Reply by date] whether you would like to renew. To accept, sign below and return a copy to us, and we will send the renewal for signature. If you plan to move out instead, please give written notice as your lease requires.

If you have any questions, please contact [Landlord contact name] at [Landlord phone] or [Landlord email].

Sincerely,

____________________________

[Landlord name]

I accept the renewal offered above.

Questions

When should I send a lease renewal letter?

Sixty to ninety days before the lease ends is common. If the rent is going up, allow at least the notice your state requires for a rent increase, and more if the lease asks for it.

Can I raise the rent at renewal?

Usually, yes, when the lease term ends. Some cities have rent control, and a few states, including California and Oregon, cap yearly increases for many rental homes. The rent increase letter explains the notice rules in more detail.

What if the tenant does not reply?

Follow up in writing before the reply date passes. In many places a tenant who stays on after the lease ends, with the landlord accepting rent, becomes a month-to-month tenant, so decide what you want and confirm it in writing.
