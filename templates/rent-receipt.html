Questions

Does a landlord have to give a rent receipt?

In some states, yes. New York, for example, requires a written receipt for rent paid in cash or any way other than a personal check, and in many places a tenant can ask for one. A receipt for every payment is good practice anyway.

What should a rent receipt include?

The date, the amount, who paid, the property, the period the rent covers, how it was paid, who received it, and any balance still owed.

Does accepting partial rent affect an eviction?

In some states, accepting partial rent can affect an eviction that has already started. The receipt records that the balance is still owed, but check the rules before accepting a partial payment during an eviction.
