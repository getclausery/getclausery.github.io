NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Effective date] between [Discloser name], [Discloser address] (the "Disclosing Party"), and [Recipient name], [Recipient address] (the "Receiving Party").

1. Purpose

The Disclosing Party will share confidential information with the Receiving Party for the purpose of [Purpose] (the "Purpose").

2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information the Disclosing Party shares in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. It does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party, that the Receiving Party already lawfully knew, or that it develops independently.

3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

  1. Use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose.
  2. Not disclose it to anyone except its employees and advisers who need to know it and are bound by similar confidentiality duties.
  3. Protect it with at least reasonable care.
  4. Return or destroy it on request and confirm in writing that it has done so.

4. Term

The obligations in this Agreement last for [Term years] years from the date above. Information that is a trade secret under applicable law stays protected for as long as it remains a trade secret.

5. No licence

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in the Confidential Information other than to use it for the Purpose.

6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law].

Signatures

[Discloser name]

By: ____________________________

Name and title: [Discloser signatory]

[Recipient name]

By: ____________________________

Name and title: [Recipient signatory]

Questions

When should I use a one-way NDA instead of a mutual one?

Use a one-way NDA when only you are sharing confidential information. If both sides will share, use the mutual NDA template.

How long should confidentiality last?

Two to five years is common for business information; trade secrets are often protected for as long as they remain secret. You choose the number of years.

Is it really free?

Yes. The download is free, and filling it in with the Clausery app is free too, with unlimited documents. Library templates never count towards a plan limit, and no account or card is needed.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Clausery runs entirely in your browser. Your answers and the finished document are created and stored on your own device.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are general samples. Laws differ between countries and states, so have the wording reviewed for your situation before you rely on it.
