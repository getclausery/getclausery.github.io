Questions

What can I ask on a rental application?

Identity, rental history, income and references are standard. Do not ask about race, religion, national origin, sex, disability, familial status or any other protected characteristic. Some states and cities also limit questions about criminal records, eviction records or immigration status, so check the rules where the property is.

Why is there no Social Security number field?

Screening companies usually collect it directly from the applicant when they run a credit check, so it does not need to sit in a document on your computer. If you do collect it, store it securely and delete it when you no longer need it.

How much can I charge for an application fee?

It depends on the state. Some cap the fee or limit it to the actual cost of screening, and some require a refund of any part not used. The fee paragraph is optional, and you can switch it off.
