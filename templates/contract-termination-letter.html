What is in the contract termination letter

  • Recipient and the contract being ended
  • For convenience, or for cause with the breach and cure notice
  • Termination clause relied on
  • Effective date and notice period
  • Optional final payment or invoice terms

[Letter date]

[Recipient name]

[Recipient address]

Re: Termination of [Contract name] dated [Contract date]

Dear [Recipient contact name],

We are writing to terminate the [Contract name] between [Sender company] and [Recipient name] dated [Contract date] (the "Contract") for cause, under [Termination clause reference] of the Contract. [Breach description] We gave you written notice of this breach on [Breach notice date], and it has not been fixed within the cure period the Contract allows.We are writing to give notice that [Sender company] is terminating the [Contract name] between us dated [Contract date] (the "Contract"), as [Termination clause reference] of the Contract allows.

The termination takes effect on [Termination effective date], which is [Notice period days] days after the date of this letter, as the Contract requires. Until then, both of us will continue to perform the Contract as usual, except as our rights allow.

[Final payment terms]

By [Materials return date], please return or securely destroy our confidential information and materials, including [Materials description], and confirm in writing that you have done so. We will do the same for yours.

The provisions of the Contract that are meant to survive termination, such as confidentiality, payment of amounts already due and limits on liability, remain in effect. This letter does not waive any of our rights or remedies under the Contract or the law, all of which are reserved.

Please direct any questions to [Sender contact name] at [Sender contact email].

Sincerely,

[Sender signatory]

[Sender signatory title], [Sender company]

Questions

How do I terminate a contract by letter?

Check the contract's termination clause first: it says who can end it, the notice period, and how notice must be given. Then send a letter that names the contract, the clause, the effective date and what happens next.

What is the difference between termination for cause and for convenience?

For cause means ending the contract because the other side breached it, usually after a chance to fix it. For convenience means ending it without a reason, as the contract allows, usually on notice.

How should I send the letter?

However the contract's notices clause requires, often by email and post or courier to a named address. Keep proof of when it was sent.
