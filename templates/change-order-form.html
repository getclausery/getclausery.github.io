What is in the change order form

  • Change order number, date and project
  • The original contract and parties
  • Description of the change
  • List of change items with amounts
  • Price increase or decrease and new total
  • Optional payment terms for the change
  • Schedule change and new completion date
  • Everything else unchanged; signatures before work starts

Questions

What is a change order?

A short signed document that changes the scope, price or schedule of an existing contract, so both sides agree the extra work and its cost before it is done.

When should I use a change order?

Whenever the client asks for something outside the agreed scope, or the work changes enough to affect the price or deadline. Agreeing it first avoids disputes at invoice time.

Does a change order replace the contract?

No. It changes only what it says; the rest of the original contract and earlier change orders stay in force.
