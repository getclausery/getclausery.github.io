Questions

What should a job description include?

The title, where and how the person will work, who they report to, a short summary of the role, the main responsibilities, the requirements, the pay range where you can share it, the benefits and how to apply.

Do I have to include the pay range?

In a growing number of places, yes. Several US states and cities, including California, Colorado, Washington, New York and Illinois, require pay ranges in job postings for many employers.

How long should a job description be?

Long enough to answer a candidate's first questions and short enough to read on a phone: usually 5 to 8 responsibilities and requirements each. Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves so good candidates are not put off.
