MUTUAL HOLD HARMLESS AGREEMENT

This Hold Harmless Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [Agreement date] between [First party name], [First party address] ("[First party short]"), and [Second party name], [Second party address] ("[Second party short]").

1. Background

[First party short] will [Activity description] at [Activity location] (the "Activity"), and the parties want to agree who bears the risk of claims arising from it.

2. Hold harmless and indemnity

[First party short] agrees to hold harmless and indemnify [Second party short], and its owners, officers, employees and agents, against all claims, demands, losses, damages, liabilities, fines and costs, including reasonable legal fees, arising out of the Activity or out of any act or omission of [First party short], its employees, guests or contractors in connection with it.

Each party agrees to hold harmless and indemnify the other, and the other's owners, officers, employees and agents, against all claims, demands, losses, damages, liabilities, fines and costs, including reasonable legal fees, to the extent they arise out of the indemnifying party's own acts or omissions, or those of its employees, guests or contractors, in connection with the Activity.

The indemnifying party will also defend any claim covered by this section at its own cost, using lawyers reasonably acceptable to the other party.

3. Exceptions

No party is required to indemnify another for loss caused by that other party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement, or for anything the law does not allow to be indemnified.

4. Insurance

[First party short]Each party will keep general liability insurance of at least [Insurance amount] per occurrence for the duration of the Activity, naming [Second party short]the other party as an additional insured, and will provide a certificate of insurance on request.Neither party is required by this Agreement to carry insurance, but each is responsible for any insurance it needs.

5. Notice of claims

A party seeking to be indemnified must tell the other party promptly in writing about any claim, give it reasonable help in dealing with the claim, and not settle the claim without its consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld.

6. Term

This Agreement covers the Activity from [Agreement date] until [End date] until the Activity ends, and continues to apply afterwards to claims arising from anything that happened during that time.

7. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law]. If any part of it cannot be enforced, it will be enforced to the greatest extent the law allows and the rest remains in effect. It is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject and can only be changed in writing signed by both.

Questions

What does hold harmless mean?

One party agrees not to hold the other responsible for certain losses, and usually to pay for claims that other people bring. For example, a company renting a venue agrees to cover claims from guests injured at its event.

Is a hold harmless agreement enforceable?

Generally yes, if it is clear, both parties sign it and it does not go against public policy. Courts usually refuse to enforce one for gross negligence or deliberate wrongdoing, and many US states limit these clauses in construction contracts, so check local law for building work.

How is it different from a liability waiver?

A liability waiver is signed by a participant to give up their own right to sue. A hold harmless agreement usually goes further: one party also covers the other against claims made by third parties.
