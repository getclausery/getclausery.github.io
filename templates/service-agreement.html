Read the full template

This is the complete wording. [Labels] are filled in from your answers, and highlighted text only appears when it applies; hover over it to see when.

SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is made on [Effective date] between [Provider name] (the "Provider") and [Customer name] (the "Customer").

1. Services

The Provider will provide the following services: [Services description].

Repeated for each service item

  • [Item]: [Price]

2. Payment

The Customer will pay the Provider's invoices within [Payment days] days. Late payments incur a fee of [Late fee terms]. A deposit of [Deposit amount] is payable on signing.

3. Term

This Agreement starts on [Start date] and renews automatically every [Renewal period] unless either party gives [Notice days] days' notice.ends on [End date].

4. Standard of work

The Provider will perform the services with reasonable skill and care. The Customer will provide the access and information the Provider reasonably needs.

5. Liability

Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim, except where the law does not allow liability to be limited.

6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing law].

[Provider name]: ____________________________

[Customer name]: ____________________________

Questions

Can the agreement renew automatically?

Yes. Choose auto-renewal, set the renewal period and the notice needed to cancel.

Can I list several services with prices?

Yes. Add as many service items as you need; each gets its own line.
