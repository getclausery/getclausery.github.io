What is in the notice to vacate (tenant)

  • Date, landlord and property address
  • Move-out date
  • End of lease, or the number of days' notice given
  • Optional co-tenants who sign too
  • Rent paid up to the move-out date
  • Optional request for a move-out inspection
  • Keys and other access devices returned
  • Security deposit amount and forwarding address
  • Contact details and signatures

[Landlord name]

[Landlord address]

Re: Notice to vacate [Property address]

Dear [Landlord name],

This letter is my written notice that I will move out of [Property address] and end my tenancy on [Move out date]. My co-tenants, [Co tenant names], are also moving out and sign this notice with me.

My lease ends on [Lease end date], and I will not be renewing it.I am giving you [Notice days] days' notice, as my rental agreement requires. I will pay rent in full up to the move-out date.

Please let me know a time to inspect the property with me before I leave, so that we can agree on its condition.

I will return all keys and [Access devices] on or before [Move out date]. Please return my security deposit of [Deposit amount], with an itemized list of any deductions, to this forwarding address: [Forwarding address]

You can reach me at [Tenant phone] or [Tenant email]. Thank you for having me as a tenant.

Sincerely,

____________________________

[Tenant name]

____________________________ [Co tenant names]

Questions

How much notice does a tenant have to give?

Whatever the lease says. For a month-to-month tenancy, many US states require at least 30 days or one full rental period, and some count the notice from the next rent due date. Check your lease and your state or city rules before you send it.

How should I deliver the notice?

In the way the lease says notices must be given. If it does not say, hand it over or send it by a method with proof of delivery, such as certified mail, and keep a copy. Some leases also accept email.

Is this the same as an eviction notice?

No. This letter is from a tenant who is choosing to leave. A landlord who wants to end a tenancy usually has to use a formal notice with wording and timing set by law, so do not use this letter for that.
