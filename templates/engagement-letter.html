An engagement letter that sets out scope, the team, fees and billing for a new matter. It supports hourly or flat fees, an optional retainer, and a list of attorneys with their rates.

What is in the engagement letter

  • Client and matter details
  • Scope of the engagement
  • Team: any number of attorneys with role and hourly rate
  • Hourly or flat fee
  • Optional advance fee deposit held in trust
  • Costs, billing and payment terms
  • Termination
  • Client acceptance block

Read the full template

This is the complete wording. [Labels] are filled in from your answers, and highlighted text only appears when it applies; hover over it to see when.

ENGAGEMENT LETTER

[Letter date]

[Client name]

[Client address]

Re: [Matter description]

Dear [Client salutation],

Thank you for choosing [Firm name] (the "Firm") to represent you. This letter confirms the terms of our engagement.

Scope of services

The Firm will represent [Client name] in connection with [Matter description] (the "Matter"). Our engagement is limited to the Matter and does not include any other legal work unless agreed in writing.

Team

The following attorneys are expected to work on the Matter:

Repeated for each attorney

  • [Name], [Role] — [Rate] per hour

Fees

Our fees are based on the time spent on the Matter at the hourly rates listed above, billed in increments of one tenth of an hour.Our fee for the Matter is a flat fee of [Flat fee], payable [Flat fee terms].

Before we begin work, we require an advance fee deposit (retainer) of [Retainer amount], which will be held in our client trust account and applied to invoices as they are issued.No advance deposit is required for this Matter.

Costs and billing

You agree to reimburse the Firm for costs incurred on your behalf, including filing fees, court reporter fees and travel. Invoices are issued monthly and are due within [Payment days] days.

Termination

You may terminate this engagement at any time by written notice. The Firm may withdraw as permitted by the applicable rules of professional conduct.

If these terms are acceptable, please sign and return a copy of this letter.

Sincerely,

[Responsible attorney]

[Firm name]

AGREED AND ACCEPTED:

____________________________

[Client name]

Date: ______________

Questions

Does this meet my state bar's requirements?

It is a general sample. Rules on engagement letters, fees and trust accounts vary by jurisdiction; adapt the wording to your rules before use.

Can I list more than one attorney?

Yes. The attorney list repeats for as many people as you add, each with a role and rate.
