eForms alternative: fill in documents without an account

Reviewed by Clausery editors · 24 October 2026

eForms is a legal-form library and document service. Clausery focuses on turning general Word templates into reusable local questionnaires. First decide whether you need an existing blank or prescribed form, or a document you will assemble repeatedly.

Compare a blank form with a reusable questionnaire

The useful distinction in an eForms comparison is often the starting file. A blank form can be exactly what you need if you intend to type into it once or complete a prescribed layout. A reusable questionnaire solves a different problem: the same names and dates appear in several places, optional paragraphs must disappear, or a table needs a varying number of rows.

Take a form you are allowed to adapt and inspect its format. Clausery accepts Word .docx templates; it is not a PDF form-field filler, OCR service or automatic PDF-to-Word converter. A scanned signature page or fixed government PDF should not be recreated casually just to fit a different app.

  • Confirm the correct transaction and jurisdiction before editing any legal wording.
  • If a suitable Word version is available, tag the changing details and preserve the required layout and instructions.
  • Test an optional section both ways and compare the completed file with the blank form and any official requirements.

For a general example, the bill of sale template demonstrates conditional details without claiming to replace a motor-vehicle authority's required form. The template tag checker helps find unmatched Word tags before you build the questionnaire. If a blank PDF already meets the need, there may be no reason to change tools. Use automation when repeat drafting and conditional wording are the problem, not merely because another download format exists.

Choose eForms if

  • An available blank form already matches the requirement
  • You need a prescribed layout rather than a new questionnaire
  • Its form and format are suitable for the intended authority

Choose Clausery if

  • Repeat data entry and optional clauses are the actual problem
  • You have a Word template you are authorised to adapt
  • The required document is not a fixed PDF form

Before moving a document from eForms

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for eForms's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and eForms together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep eForms or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with eForms?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check eForms's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Can Clausery fill an existing PDF form?

No. It assembles Word .docx templates. You can print the resulting document to PDF, but this is different from importing a fixed PDF and completing its existing form fields.

Does a general bill of sale replace an official vehicle form?

Not necessarily. A registering authority may require its own form, title transfer, disclosures or other steps. Check the applicable requirements rather than assuming a general template is sufficient.
