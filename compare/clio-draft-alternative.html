Clio Draft alternative for firms that cannot upload client files

Reviewed by Clausery editors · 24 October 2026 · 5 min read

Clio Draft is a document-automation option in the Clio ecosystem. Clausery is independent of practice-management software and assembles Word files locally. Check what you would lose in matter-data reuse before comparing the document editors alone.

Separate matter-data reuse from the document itself#

If your practice already uses Clio, evaluate the value of its matter data before comparing template editors. Entering a client address once and reusing it across a matter is not the same as entering an address into a single Word questionnaire. Clausery has no connection to Clio Manage and does not automatically fetch contacts, matter references or filing information.

Take an authorised Word version of one routine letter. List every detail that currently comes from your practice-management system, including addresses, responsible lawyers and matter numbers. Decide who would enter or transfer each value in a local process, and how changes would be checked. Do not assume similar-looking field names create a live link between the systems.

  1. Build the letter in Clausery using fictional matter details and retain the original letter for comparison.
  2. Change the client address and regenerate. Check the body, headers, signature block and any repeated references.
  3. Record where the final Word file will be stored and how colleagues will know which version is approved.

For staff using the same wording, template packs distribute the questionnaire and Word file; they do not synchronise client records. For facts handed from one person to another, use an answers export with appropriate confidentiality controls. Keep official court forms and filing requirements outside this test unless a qualified reviewer has confirmed the specific document. A local engagement letter can be a useful independent drafting aid without replacing a firm's matter-management workflow.

Choose Clio Draft if#

  • Reusing matter information from Clio is a central requirement
  • A required form or workflow is covered by your selected service
  • You want drafting administered alongside practice management

Choose Clausery if#

  • You need a drafting tool that works independently of Clio
  • Manual entry or controlled file handoffs fit the matter process
  • Keeping document contents on firm devices is the priority

Before moving a template from Clio Draft#

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for Clio Draft's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery#

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and Clio Draft together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep Clio Draft or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with Clio Draft?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check Clio Draft's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Does Clausery pull client or matter details from Clio Manage?

No. There is no Clio integration or automatic matter-data synchronisation. Enter the answers locally or use supported answers files and spreadsheets with a deliberate review process.

Does Clausery replace a court-form library?

No. The library contains general Word templates, not an official jurisdiction-specific court-form service. Use the required official form or a suitable legal platform when a filing must follow a prescribed format.
