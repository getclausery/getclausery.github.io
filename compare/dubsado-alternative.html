Dubsado alternative: contracts without the setup

Dubsado is a client-management and workflow platform. Clausery does not recreate that automation; it turns Word templates into local questionnaires. An occasional contract and an automated client journey are different requirements.

Inventory every trigger before replacing an automated workflow

For a service business considering Dubsado, make a list of the actions that happen without someone remembering to do them. A completed inquiry might trigger a response, appointment link, contract or follow-up. A Word generator is not a replacement for that chain just because it produces one of the documents in it.

Choose one existing client process and draw each step with its owner. Mark whether the step is automatic, manual or dependent on a payment or signature. Clausery does not watch an inbox, schedule appointments, send reminders or detect that a client completed an external action. Every such dependency needs a deliberate new home.

  • Separate approved contract wording from the workflow that chooses when to send it.
  • Use a fictional project to check optional terms and the handoff to your existing signing service.
  • If using client intake, test downloading and returning the answers file; do not treat it as a hosted lead form or automatic CRM update.

The file-based intake guide explains that distinction. A service agreement provides a small drafting trial without rebuilding the whole business process. Keep existing scheduling and reminder systems until the replacement process is proven. If you need only occasional editable contracts, local drafting can be enough; if most value comes from the triggers around those contracts, assess the complete workflow before changing a subscription.

Choose Dubsado if

  • Automatic actions after inquiries, payments or signing are essential
  • The client portal and scheduling are part of your normal service
  • You need a managed workflow, not just the document inside it

Choose Clausery if

  • A short manual handoff is appropriate for the volume of work
  • You want to maintain contract wording in an editable Word master
  • A hosted lead form or CRM is not part of the requirement

Before moving a template from Dubsado

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for Dubsado's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and Dubsado together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep Dubsado or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with Dubsado?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check Dubsado's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Does a Clausery intake form automatically update a CRM?

No. The client saves an answers file and sends it back. You import it into a draft. Clausery has no hosted submissions inbox, webhooks or automatic CRM synchronisation.

Will Clausery send reminders when a contract is unsigned?

No. Signature collection, status tracking and reminders belong in your external signing or workflow system. Clausery prepares the document but does not monitor what happens after it is sent.
