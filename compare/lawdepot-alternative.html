Check jurisdictional coverage before comparing download costs

A free editable file and a document selected for a particular jurisdiction are different benefits. If you are evaluating LawDepot for a lease, first list the disclosures, deposit rules and tenancy terms that apply to the property. Clausery's residential lease is a general sample. It does not become state-specific because you put a state name in an address field.

Compare the scope of the actual documents, not just the download button. Is the form intended for the correct transaction and location? Who is responsible for checking mandatory language? Does the final version include all schedules and disclosures? A lower software cost does not compensate for using an unsuitable legal document.

  • Choose one transaction and identify whether a prescribed form or local professional review is required.
  • Check the vendor's current terms for available formats, charges, renewal conditions and the right to reuse the wording.
  • If you have an authorised Word file, retain its approved legal text while replacing only the changing facts with tags.

The residential lease checklist explains the questions to resolve before using the general lease template. For a document outside the library, do not force a roughly similar template to do the job. Keep the appropriate jurisdiction-specific document or obtain advice, and use Clausery only for a drafting process that has been approved for your situation.

Choose LawDepot if

  • Its selected document covers a location or transaction you need
  • The provider’s workflow and document terms fit your requirements
  • The form is outside Clausery’s general template library

Choose Clausery if

  • A reviewed general template is appropriate for the transaction
  • You need an editable Word file and repeated local drafting
  • You are prepared to check mandatory local terms separately

Before moving a document from LawDepot

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for LawDepot's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and LawDepot together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep LawDepot or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with LawDepot?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check LawDepot's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Are Clausery templates tailored automatically to my state?

No. They are general samples that you must adapt and review for the transaction and jurisdiction. Entering a state or country does not add every required disclosure or make the wording locally compliant.

Can I automate a Word document I downloaded elsewhere?

Yes, if you are entitled to use and adapt it. Add tags to an authorised .docx copy and review the questionnaire and output. Clausery does not bypass another provider’s download charges or licence terms.
