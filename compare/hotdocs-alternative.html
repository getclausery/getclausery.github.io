HotDocs alternative: lightweight document automation in the browser

HotDocs is associated with complex document-assembly deployments and specialist authoring. Clausery is a smaller Word-template tool with no server component. Assess the logic and operational requirements of your document set before treating one as a replacement for the other.

Test the difficult branches before migrating a document set

A large HotDocs deployment can embody years of drafting decisions, not just a folder of Word documents. Before replacing any part of it, inventory the component logic, shared variables, interview dependencies and documents produced together. Clausery does not import a HotDocs component library or reproduce an enterprise assembly workflow automatically.

Select one self-contained document rather than the most interconnected set. Create a small acceptance matrix: one party and several parties; an optional guarantee on and off; a missing amount; a date at a month boundary; and an empty repeating list. Keep an approved expected result for each case. A successful ordinary example is not evidence that the exceptions work.

  • Translate a condition into the documented expression language, then check its true, false and unanswered states.
  • Inspect numbering and table rows when a section disappears or a list grows. Compare the downloaded Word file, not only the browser preview.
  • Assign ownership for template updates, regression checks and distribution of the approved version.

The logic reference defines the operators and functions available in Clausery, while the repeating-table guide covers the row structure. Reject a migration if a critical rule has no suitable representation or the organisation depends on central administration that is not being replaced. Local assembly may fit a smaller subset of routine forms, but it should not be presented as a drop-in conversion of a complex enterprise installation.

Choose HotDocs if

  • Your documents depend on a large shared component library
  • Central administration and enterprise integrations are essential
  • Specialist authors maintain a tested assembly system

Choose Clausery if

  • A self-contained subset of documents fits the available Word tags
  • Authors prefer to keep approved wording in ordinary Word files
  • You do not need a central assembly service for this task

Before moving a template from HotDocs

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for HotDocs's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and HotDocs together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep HotDocs or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with HotDocs?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check HotDocs's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Can Clausery open a HotDocs component library?

No. Use an authorised original Word document and rebuild the questions and logic with Clausery tags and expressions. A component library and its integration code do not convert automatically.

How should we validate a converted template?

Compare downloaded Word outputs with approved examples for ordinary and exceptional inputs. Include optional clauses, repeating groups, missing values, calculations and dates. Keep a reviewer responsible for the resulting legal wording.
