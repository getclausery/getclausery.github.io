Separate Word document quality from sales approvals and signatures

For a sales team evaluating PandaDoc, define where the document ends and the deal workflow begins. A quote may need approved prices, a legal review, a customer signature and a CRM status update. Clausery can assemble an editable Word quote or contract, but it does not route approval requests, track document opens or update a sales pipeline.

Take a quotation with two line items, an optional service and a tax calculation. Generate the ordinary version and the version without that service. Inspect totals, page breaks, headings and the final file in Word. This tests document assembly. It says nothing about whether a customer has viewed, accepted or signed the offer.

  1. Name the person who approves prices and non-standard terms before a document is sent.
  2. Choose an external signing route if an electronic signature and audit evidence are required.
  3. Record where the signed version and approval record are filed and who updates the deal status.

Use the free Word quote template for the numbers and the questionnaire designer instructions for the questions. Do not describe this as a replacement for configure-price-quote software or an integrated approval service. It can suit a business whose approved workflow already handles those steps and needs editable files, while a team relying on connected sales automation should compare the full service rather than only the per-document drafting cost.

Choose PandaDoc if

  • A connected signing and approval service is the main requirement
  • The sales pipeline depends on document events and CRM updates
  • Your team needs the workflow around a proposal, not only its file

Choose Clausery if

  • Approved prices and terms are already handled outside the generator
  • You need editable Word output from a local questionnaire
  • Free library drafting fits, with Pro optional and priced per user

Before moving a template from PandaDoc

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for PandaDoc's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and PandaDoc together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep PandaDoc or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with PandaDoc?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check PandaDoc's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Does Clausery include electronic signatures or document-open tracking?

No. Send the Word file or a printed PDF through an appropriate external signing service. Keep the signed document and audit evidence there; Clausery does not track views or signing status.

Can Clausery calculate a quote without a paid sales platform?

The free quote template calculates line items and tax locally and produces editable Word output. That is not a CRM connection, product catalogue, approval workflow or automatic quote-to-payment system.
