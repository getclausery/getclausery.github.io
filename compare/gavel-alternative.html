Gavel alternative that keeps client data on your computer

Gavel focuses on legal document automation and client workflows. Clausery offers a narrower alternative: assemble Word documents on your own computer, without uploading the document contents. The important comparison is the whole intake-to-document process.

Decide whether you are replacing drafting or a hosted intake workflow

For a firm comparing Gavel with a local drafting tool, the first question is who collects the answers. A lawyer completing an engagement letter on a laptop is a different job from inviting a client into a hosted interview and routing the result to colleagues. Clausery addresses the first directly. Its optional client intake feature is a downloadable HTML form, not a continuously available client portal.

Try a small engagement containing a retainer and a list of lawyers. Use fictional details, produce a version with the retainer and one without, and compare the actual Word output. Then ask how the answers will reach the lawyer. If a client needs a web account, live status updates or an automated handoff, do not count a file attachment as an equivalent replacement.

  • Map each handoff: who sends the questionnaire, receives the answers, checks them and files the finished document?
  • Check whether security policy permits an unencrypted answers file to travel by email, or requires an approved transfer channel.
  • Identify any matter creation, notifications or payment steps that would still need another system.

The client intake instructions show the full file-based round trip, including what is and is not inside the form. Use the engagement letter template for a drafting test. If hosted intake is central to the service your firm offers, keeping that workflow and using Clausery for a limited set of local documents may be safer than an all-at-once switch.

Choose Gavel if

  • A hosted client interview is part of the service your firm delivers
  • You depend on supported matter-management integrations
  • You have approved the provider and deployment for client data

Choose Clausery if

  • The immediate task is a local Word document, not a hosted workflow
  • Client instructions restrict uploading document contents
  • Your firm can manage the remaining handoffs explicitly

Before moving a template from Gavel

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for Gavel's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and Gavel together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep Gavel or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with Gavel?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check Gavel's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Can a client fill in a Clausery questionnaire without a portal?

Yes, with Pro client intake. You send a self-contained HTML form; the client saves an answers JSON file and returns it through an agreed channel. You import it into a draft. There is no hosted inbox, client login or automatic return of answers.

Will a Gavel workflow import directly into Clausery?

There is no Gavel workflow importer. Start from a Word document you are entitled to use, add Clausery tags and recreate the relevant conditions. Test each path and keep any external workflow steps separately.
