Decide whether you need drafting software or professional advice

When considering a Rocket Lawyer membership, separate access to legal help from the task of producing a file. Clausery is software, not a law firm. Its free templates and questionnaires do not include an attorney reviewing your terms, advising on a dispute or accepting responsibility for a document's suitability.

For a straightforward recurring letter, a reviewed Word master may be enough. For a settlement, a disputed termination or a transaction whose consequences you do not understand, obtaining advice is a separate decision. Do not treat a free download as a substitute for a legal service you actually need.

  1. List what you used during a typical month: drafting, legal questions, signing, storage and any business services.
  2. Check which of those services your proposed replacement really supplies, and confirm the provider's current membership and cancellation terms directly.
  3. Test a non-sensitive document in Clausery, then identify who will review, sign and retain the finished version.

The service agreement template can illustrate the drafting process. The liability-clause explanation also shows why selecting a clause involves judgment rather than simply filling every blank. Keep existing signed records and any signature evidence in their original storage system. Clausery outputs editable Word files and can print to PDF, but it does not transfer a legal-service relationship or recreate an external signature audit trail.

Choose Rocket Lawyer if

  • You want a qualifying legal-help service as well as drafting
  • A selected membership covers services you regularly use
  • You need advice about the transaction rather than only a template

Choose Clausery if

  • Approved wording and local document generation cover the task
  • You arrange any legal review and signing separately
  • You want the editable file without a drafting membership

Before moving a document from Rocket Lawyer

  1. Keep existing signed records and any external audit evidence. Work from an authorised editable Word master, not the only copy of a signed document.
  2. Replace changing facts with {tags} and check them with the Word template checker. There is no automatic importer for Rocket Lawyer's rules or workflow.
  3. Build and test the questionnaire, including optional clauses and unusual inputs. Compare the downloaded .docx with your approved wording.
  4. Assign responsibility for any signing, legal review, payments, client communication and filing that remain outside the document generator.

For repeat drafting, use the tag syntax and keep a workspace backup. Move a limited, reviewed process first; do not cancel another service until the required records and replacement workflow are in place.

What stays outside Clausery

Clausery prepares Word documents, including contracts, invoices, quotes and receipts. It does not collect signatures or payments, provide legal advice, maintain a hosted client portal or synchronise a CRM. Keep the systems needed for those tasks.

Questions

Can I use Clausery and Rocket Lawyer together?

Yes, where the separate roles fit your process. Clausery creates editable Word documents locally; it does not need an integration to produce a file. Keep Rocket Lawyer or another suitable service for the workflow, advice or record-keeping that you still need.

How should I compare Clausery pricing with Rocket Lawyer?

Clausery library templates are free, plus 3 of your own templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month, with an optional 14-day trial that needs no card and simply ends. Free library output includes a small Made with Clausery line; Pro removes it. Check Rocket Lawyer's official site for current prices, limits, billing terms and the services included.

Does Clausery include attorney consultations or legal review?

No. It supplies drafting software and general templates. A lawyer’s advice, local review and any legal-service relationship must be arranged separately.

Will moving a document preserve its electronic signature evidence?

Not as a new Clausery record. Keep the original signed document and the signing provider’s audit evidence. An editable Word copy for future drafting is not a replacement for that signed record.
