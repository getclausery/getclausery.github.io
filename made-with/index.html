The document you received was made with Clausery

Clausery makes invoices, quotes, receipts and contracts in Word for small businesses and freelancers. Answer a few questions and download a finished document you can still edit, with the totals and tax worked out. It is free, with no sign-up.

Make an invoice, freeBrowse 87 free templates

A question about the document itself? Amounts, due dates, payments and terms are set by the business that sent it, so please contact them. Clausery never sees the documents people make: each one is created in the sender's own browser and nothing is uploaded.

Free, with no account

Every one of the 87 library templates can be downloaded or filled in at no cost, with unlimited documents.

The maths done for you

Line items, discounts, tax and balances are calculated, and your next invoice continues the numbering with your details already filled in.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Your clients' details and your documents are never uploaded to a server.
