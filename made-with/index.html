The document you received was made with Clausery

Clausery is free, open-source software that makes invoices, quotes, receipts and contracts as editable Word files. Totals, tax and numbering are worked out automatically — and the sender's document contents never leave their browser. This is not clausery.ai (the separate enterprise compliance company); it is an independent document-generation tool used by the business that sent you the file.

A question about the document itself? Amounts, due dates, bank details, payments and terms are set by the business that sent it, so please contact them. Clausery never sees the documents people make: each one is created in the sender's own browser and nothing is uploaded.

Three things receivers often ask

Is this a scam? Is it safe to pay?

Clausery is document-generation software, not a payment service. The document was sent to you by the business named on it; amounts, due dates, bank details and payment terms are set by them. Verify the sender (reply to an email you already trust, or call the number on file) and pay using your usual channels. Clausery does not handle payments.

Can I edit the document?

Yes — the file is a normal .docx you can open in Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice or Pages, edit, save, or print to PDF. Nothing is locked.

Does this track me?

No. The document is just a Word file; there are no pixels, beacons or hidden trackers added by Clausery. The link to this page is a plain text hyperlink at the bottom of free-plan documents.

Free, with no account

Every one of the 87 library templates can be filled in at no cost, with unlimited documents and no sign-up.

The maths done for you

Line items, discounts, tax and balances are calculated automatically, and the next invoice continues the sender's numbering.

Private by design

Everything runs in the sender's browser. Client details and document contents are never uploaded to a server.
