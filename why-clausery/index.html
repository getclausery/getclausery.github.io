Why Clausery

Most tools that make invoices, quotes and contracts want your documents, or your time, or both. Clausery does neither: it makes a Word file on your own computer, gives the whole library away for free, and publishes the code so you can check it.

Proof you can check yourself

Trust is easier to judge from evidence than from adjectives. These are the checks this site is built to survive:

  • Read the code. The document engine, the app and this website are published on GitHub, so you can see what runs when you press a button.
  • Watch every release. Each version is listed with what changed in the changelog, including the ones that fixed something.
  • Watch the network. The security overview lists every request the app makes, and the privacy policy lists every data flow, in plain words.
  • Host it yourself. The self-hosting guide covers running a copy on your own domain or intranet if your policies require it.
  • Run the checks. The same build and test suite that produces this site is public, so the claims above can be reproduced rather than taken on trust.
  • Leave if it does not fit. Start free with no card, try Pro for 14 days, and if it still is not right, ask for a refund within 30 days. The details are on the pricing page.

What Clausery does not do

Being clear about the edges is more useful than a longer feature list. Clausery does not:

  • collect payments, or act as a bank;
  • provide electronic signatures, so sign with the service you already use or on paper;
  • host a client portal, schedule meetings or keep a CRM;
  • give legal, tax or financial advice, and it is not a law firm;
  • promise a document is right for your jurisdiction: the templates are general samples you review and adapt.

What remains outside Clausery is a decision about your own process, not a missing feature. The documentation explains the handoffs, and the about page covers the commercial model.

What it costs

The library is free: all 87 Word templates, 24 clause explanations, the guides and 9 calculators, with no sign-up. Automating up to 3 of your own templates is free too. Pro is $19 per user per month ($190 per year) and adds unlimited own templates, calculations in them, documents from a spreadsheet, an encrypted workspace and client intake forms. Team is $49 a month for five seats. The pricing page has the full plan matrix.

Questions

Is Clausery free to use?

Yes for the whole library. All 87 Word templates, 24 clause explanations, the guides and 9 calculators are free, with no sign-up and no time limit. You can also automate up to 3 of your own Word templates free. Pro is $19 per user per month when you want more.

What does Clausery do that other invoicing tools do not?

The output is a normal Word file you keep editing, the document is assembled on your own device rather than on a vendor server, and there is no account to create before you start. A free plan that does not expire is also unusual among tools that put the library behind a trial.

Do I have to sign up or give a card?

No. Every library template can be downloaded or filled in without an account. The 14-day Pro trial starts in the app with no card, nothing renews and you keep everything you made.

What if it is not right for me?

Email within 30 days of your first payment and the amount is refunded in full, with no form and no reason required. The Free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to cancel there either.

Can my clients see my documents or my business data?

No. Reading templates, evaluating answers and assembling the .docx happen in the browser. Document contents are not uploaded, and a saved draft stays in the browser database until you delete it or move it with a backup file.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. Clausery is software that fills in templates you can read and adapt. It is not a law firm, it does not give legal advice, and it does not collect payments. Have a document reviewed for your situation before you rely on it.
