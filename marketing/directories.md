# Where to list Clausery

Do batch 1 in week 1 and batch 2 when you have time. A listing on its own sends a trickle of visitors. The value is in being findable where people compare tools, plus a few links that help search engines trust a new domain. Rules, fees and sign-up requirements change, so check each site before you submit. Never pay for "submission to 500 directories" services: they produce spam links that can hurt rankings.

## Copy to reuse

**Name:** Clausery

**URL:** `https://getclausery.github.io/`

**One line** (about 60 characters):

```
Invoices and Word templates, filled in privately in-browser
```

**Short description** (about 160 characters):

```
Make invoices, quotes and receipts in Word with totals, tax and numbering done for you, plus 87 free templates. No sign-up; nothing you type is uploaded.
```

**Long description:**

```
Clausery helps small businesses and freelancers make invoices, quotes, receipts and contracts as editable Word (.docx) files. Answer a few questions and download a finished document: line items, discounts, tax and balances are calculated, and the next invoice starts with your details, the next number and today's date.

Everything runs in the browser. Templates, answers and documents are never uploaded, there is no account, and the app works offline once loaded.

87 Word templates are free to download or fill in online, including invoices, quotes, receipts, purchase orders, NDAs, leases and HR letters. Businesses with their own Word templates can add {tags} to turn them into guided questionnaires, with conditional clauses, repeating table rows and calculations.

Free has no time limit. Pro ($19 per user per month, or $190 a year) adds unlimited own templates, calculations in them, bulk documents from a spreadsheet, workspace encryption and client intake forms, and removes the small "Made with Clausery" line from documents. Pro has a 14-day free trial with no card and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
```

**Categories:** Invoicing, Document generation, Templates, Small business, Freelance, Productivity, Privacy

**Pricing model:** Freemium. Free plan; Pro $19/user/month or $190/year; Team $49/month for 5 seats; 14-day free trial, no card.

**Platforms:** Web (any modern browser), installable PWA, works offline

**Images:** `assets/screenshot-invoice.png`, `assets/screenshot-designer.png`, `assets/og.png` (1200×630), `assets/icon-512.png` (logo)

## Month 2: app stores (optional)

Clausery is already an installable PWA, so it can go into app stores without a rewrite. People search stores for "invoice maker".

- **Microsoft Store**: [PWABuilder](https://www.pwabuilder.com/) packages the site for the Store. Check Microsoft's current developer account terms.
- **Google Play**: PWABuilder can also build a Trusted Web Activity. It needs a Play developer account (one-time fee) and a `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` file served from the site root. Do this after any domain move, because the file is tied to the domain.
