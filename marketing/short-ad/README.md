## Why this concept wins
Research across the 2026 short-form ad benchmarks ([HeyOrca](https://www.heyorca.com/blog/the-best-social-media-hooks-for-2026), [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/best-video-ad-hooks-2026), [PxlPeak TikTok creative guide](https://pxlpeak.com/docs/tiktok-ads/creative), Reddit r/GrowthHacking hook analysis):

| Element | Why it's in the ad | Proven lift |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook:** "Stop scrolling if you send invoices" (spoken + burned-in caption at 0.0s, on a red "Sign up to download" wall) | #1 CTR pattern for SaaS — direct callout + pattern-interrupt pain visual | ~3.8% avg CTR on Meta/TikTok ([FluxNote Hook 1](https://fluxnote.io/guides/best-video-ad-hooks-2026)) |
| **3-act structure, 25s total** | Hook (0–3.5s) → Demo (3.5–17.5s) → Payoff (17.5–25.5s) — beats the 71% 3-second drop-off | ([PxlPeak](https://pxlpeak.com/docs/tiktok-ads/creative)) |
| **"Nobody is talking about / I tested" alternative chosen: live screen demo** | UGC-style product-in-use outperforms polished studio and talking-head for SaaS, and has a 10–21 day creative lifespan vs 3–7 for trend-jacking | ([PxlPeak](https://pxlpeak.com/docs/tiktok-ads/creative), [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/best-video-ad-hooks-2026)) |
| **Bold-number payoff: "87 templates, ALL FREE."** | Specific odd/non-rounded numbers outperform rounded ones by 22% | ([FluxNote Hook 4](https://fluxnote.io/guides/best-video-ad-hooks-2026)) |
| **Burned-in captions, every word** | 85%+ of short-form is watched muted; caption-on keeps 3s retention | ([PxlPeak pre-launch checklist](https://pxlpeak.com/docs/tiktok-ads/creative)) |
| **Sound-on voiceover, conversational female UGC tone** | Spoken hooks beat text-only on TikTok | ([Nimply](https://nimply.io/blog/hooks-for-social-media)) |
| **No brand intro, no logo animation** | The #1 creative mistake is a logo/intro in the first 2 seconds | ([PxlPeak](https://pxlpeak.com/docs/tiktok-ads/creative)) |
| **Native-feel safe area** | Caption pill sits below the center of the screen (~y1440–1700) to avoid the TikTok/Reels right-rail action buttons and iOS home indicator | platform spec |
| **One single CTA: "Link in bio"** | No website-in-video, no QR, no 5-line offer wall — single next step = higher save+click-through rate |

The ad makes **zero invented claims**. Every statement it makes is a verifiable feature of the product that ships:
- "makes a normal Word file" — Clausery downloads `.docx`.
- "right in your browser" — local processing, no upload.
- "No signup. No card." — library is usable without an account; free tier has no card.
- "87 templates, all free" — matches the template count in `/templates/` (verified in the current build).
- URL: `getclausery.github.io` — real, live.

## Posting settings
- **Sound:** use the video's own audio (the voiceover is the track). No trending sound overlay — it fights the voiceover and the ad won't be approved/boosted as native.
- **Cover/thumbnail:** use `cover.jpg` (the red "Stop scrolling / if you send invoices" frame). When the platform asks you to pick a cover frame, scrub to the 0.0–0.5s red frame.
- **Captions:** burned in already. Turn platform auto-captions **off** to avoid double captions.
- **Comments:** leave them on. Pin a comment from the account: "URL is getclausery.github.io — no account, no card."
- **Link in bio:** set to `https://getclausery.github.io/why-clausery/` (the page built in PR #54 — it has the comparison table and the proof points) rather than the home page; converts higher from a "I want to check if this is real" click.
- **Ad policy compliance:** no testimonials, no user counts, no "trusted by", no false urgency/countdown, no before/after income claims — passes TikTok/Reels/Meta ad policy for organic, and can be boosted later without creative edits.
