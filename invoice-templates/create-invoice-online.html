No sign-up. Editable Word (.docx) or print to PDF. Your answers stay in your browser. Updated 7 November 2026.

Creating an invoice online is faster than opening Word and typing one from a blank file every time. The template prompts you for every field an invoice should have (invoice number, dates, your details, the client, line items, totals, tax, payment terms), so you do not accidentally leave out the invoice number or the due date.

Your business details, logo and payment methods are saved in your browser so subsequent invoices start pre-filled; each new invoice suggests the next invoice number in your sequence automatically. For repeat clients you can also start a new invoice with the client details already filled in.

What to include#

  • Invoice number and dates. A unique invoice number, the invoice date, and the due date (for example NET 30 or due on receipt). The next number in your sequence is suggested automatically.
  • You and your client. Your business name, address, email and an optional tax/VAT/GST number, plus the client's name and billing address. Saved locally so you never type them twice.
  • Line items. Description of what you are charging for, quantity, unit price and amount. Line totals calculate as you type; hours-based billing works by using hours as the quantity and your hourly rate as the unit price.
  • Totals, tax and discount. Subtotal, an optional percentage discount, one tax rate (VAT, GST, sales tax or none), and the grand total. Any amount already paid (a deposit, for example) is subtracted to show the balance due.
  • How to pay. Payment methods, bank details and any late-fee wording. A warning about fake change-of-bank-details emails is included by default.

How to fill it in#

  1. Step 1: Click "Make a free invoice"

    Click the CTA below to open the invoice maker in your browser. Your business details and logo are pre-filled if you have used Clausery before.

  2. Step 2: Fill in the client and invoice numbers

    Pick a client (or add a new one), confirm today's date and the due date, and check the invoice number that was suggested for you.

  3. Step 3: Add the line items

    Enter each item or task with a description, quantity and rate. Add a separate line when the rate or the tax rate changes.

  4. Step 4: Check totals and tax

    Review subtotal, any discount, tax and total. If the client has paid a deposit, enter the amount in "Amount paid" to show the balance due.

  5. Step 5: Download and send

    Download the invoice as an editable Word file if you want to tweak the layout, or print/save as a PDF to email straight to the client.

Questions

Do I need to sign up to create an invoice?

No. You can create and download an invoice without creating an account, giving an email address, or adding a credit card. Everything runs in your browser.

Does the invoice maker add up totals for me?

Yes. Line amounts, subtotal, discount, tax and grand total update as you type. The downloaded Word file keeps the figures as plain numbers you can edit further.

Is anything I type uploaded?

No. The invoice is assembled entirely in your browser from your answers and the template; no data leaves your device unless you choose to download it or share it.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload a logo in the app and it is saved in your browser for all future invoices, appearing in the header of the finished Word or PDF file.

Can I make an invoice in Word instead of PDF?

Yes. Click "Download .docx" to get an editable Word file you can open in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice and edit further.
