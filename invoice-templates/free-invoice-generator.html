Free invoice generator

Reviewed by Clausery editors · 3 November 2026 · 4 min read

Generate an invoice in your browser in under a minute — no account, no email and no card. Answer a few questions about you, your client and what you are charging for; line totals, VAT/GST and the balance due calculate as you type. Then download the finished invoice as an editable Word file or a printable PDF.

No sign-up. Editable Word (.docx) or print to PDF. Your answers stay in your browser. Updated 3 November 2026.

An invoice generator takes the thinking out of billing. Instead of opening Word, remembering every field an invoice needs by law and doing the maths yourself, the generator prompts you for each piece (invoice number, dates, your details, the client, line items, tax, payment terms), adds up the figures, and produces a clean invoice ready to email. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded to a server.

Unlike invoice templates you download and fill by hand, a generator reuses what you have already told it. Your business name, logo, tax number, bank details and preferred payment terms are saved in your browser so the next invoice starts pre-filled; the generator also suggests the next invoice number in your sequence so numbering mistakes cannot happen.

What to include#

  • Automatic totals. Line amounts, subtotal, optional discount, tax (VAT, GST, sales tax) and grand total calculate as you type. No spreadsheet required.
  • Invoice numbering. Each new invoice suggests the next number in your sequence automatically, so you never duplicate or skip an invoice number.
  • Client and your details saved. Your business details and recent clients are stored in your browser for next time — no retyping, no sign-up.
  • Word or PDF. Download an editable .docx to tweak the layout further in Word or Google Docs, or save/print as PDF to email straight to the client.
  • Logo and branding. Upload a logo and choose an accent colour; both appear in the header of every invoice you generate.
  • Tax, discount and deposits. Apply one tax rate, an optional percentage discount, and subtract any deposit or part-payment already received to show the balance due.

How to fill it in#

  1. Step 1: Click "Generate an invoice"

    Open the free invoice generator below. Your business details are pre-filled if you have used Clausery before.

  2. Step 2: Enter the client and invoice details

    Pick a recent client or add a new one; confirm today's date, the due date and the invoice number the generator suggests.

  3. Step 3: Add your line items

    Enter each item or task with description, quantity and rate. Line totals update as you type.

  4. Step 4: Check tax and discount

    Set your tax rate and any discount. If the client has paid a deposit, enter it under Amount paid to show the balance due.

  5. Step 5: Download as Word or PDF

    Download .docx to edit the layout, or print/save as PDF to email. Send the invoice to the client using the share sheet on mobile or the pre-filled email button on desktop.

Questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. You can generate, download and send unlimited invoices without signing up, giving an email address or adding a credit card. A small "Made with Clausery" line appears on Free-plan invoices; a no-card 14-day Pro trial removes it.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. The generator runs entirely in the browser page you are on — no download, no app install, no account. It also works offline once the page has loaded.

Does it calculate VAT, GST and totals?

Yes. Line totals, subtotal, discount, tax and grand total update as you type. VAT (UK/IE), GST/HST (Canada, NZ, AU), sales tax and South African VAT are all supported by setting the right tax rate.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload a logo before downloading and it appears in the header of every generated invoice. It is saved in your browser for next time.

Is there an invoice number generator?

Yes. The generator looks at the invoices you have already made and suggests the next number in your sequence (for example INV-0043), so you do not have to remember what you were up to.

Are invoices stored online?

No. Drafts are saved only in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB); nothing is uploaded to a server. The data leaves your device only when you download the file or share it.
