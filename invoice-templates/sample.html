A sample invoice shows the structure: who it is from, who it is to, a unique invoice number, dates, what is being charged for, quantities and rates, totals, tax, payment details and terms. Looking at a completed example is the fastest way to make sure you do not miss a required field on your first real invoice.

Use this sample as a reference for layout and wording. When you are ready to bill your client, click the button below to start a real invoice pre-formatted exactly like the sample but with empty fields for you to fill in.

What to include#

  • Header with your details. Your business name, address, contact information, logo and an optional "Tax invoice" heading if you are VAT/GST registered.
  • Client (bill-to). The customer's name and billing address; optional ship-to if goods are delivered elsewhere.
  • Invoice number and dates. A unique invoice number, the issue date and the payment due date (for example NET 30).
  • Line items. A table with description, quantity, unit rate and line amount. In the sample you see an hour-based line and a fixed-fee line.
  • Totals, tax and balance. Subtotal, optional discount, tax (VAT, GST or sales tax), total, any amount already paid and the balance due.
  • Payment terms. How to pay (bank transfer, card, cheque), bank details or payment link, and optional late-fee wording.

How to fill it in#

  1. Step 1: Look over the sample

    Scan the filled-in example below to see where each piece of information goes.

  2. Step 2: Open your own invoice

    Click "Make an invoice from this sample" to start a blank invoice with the same layout.

  3. Step 3: Replace the example text

    Type in your own business details (these save in your browser for next time), your client, the invoice number and dates.

  4. Step 4: Add real line items

    Enter the items or services you are billing for, with quantity and rate; totals calculate for you.

  5. Step 5: Download and send

    Download as Word or PDF and email the invoice to your client.

Questions

Can I download the sample as Word?

Yes. Click "Download sample" or start a new invoice (blank layout matches the sample) and download as .docx, then replace the example text with your own details in Word.

Is the sample a real invoice I can use?

The names, amounts and bank details on this page are invented. Use it as a structural reference; replace every field with real information before sending.

What font and layout is the sample in?

The sample uses a clean sans-serif layout with your logo top-left, invoice number and dates top-right, bill-to and line-item table in the body, and payment details in the footer. This is the layout your real invoice will use.

Is a sample invoice the same as a proforma?

No. A standard sample is a completed sales invoice (a demand for payment). A proforma invoice is a quote-like document sent before supply; use the proforma template for that.

Do I need to include every field shown?

Required fields depend on your jurisdiction and tax registration (invoice number, dates, your and the client's details, line items, total, currency and payment information are universal; tax registration numbers are required once registered). The how-to guide lists what applies to you.
