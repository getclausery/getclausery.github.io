Free Word invoice templates by task, trade and country

Pick your trade and the invoice opens with the lines that trade usually bills, ready for your prices. Pick your country and it opens with the right tax, rate and tax number. Totals and tax are worked out for you, your next invoice remembers your details and number, and you download an editable Word file. Free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

By billing task

Request an upfront deposit, bill hours, or reconcile the final balance. Each page has a worked example and an app starter for that task.

Deposit invoice

Make a deposit invoice in Word: calculate the percentage, request only the deposit, and keep the final balance clear. Free, editable .docx, no sign-up.

Hourly invoice

Invoice hours in Word: enter work dates, tasks, hours and hourly rates, with line totals calculated. Free editable .docx for freelancers and consultants.

Final invoice

Make a final invoice in Word: show the full project price, subtract deposits actually received, and calculate the remaining balance. Free, no sign-up.
