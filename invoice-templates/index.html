Common questions

Are these invoice templates really free?

Yes. Every invoice template here is free to fill in online and download as an editable Word file or printable PDF, with no account, no sign-up and nothing uploaded. You can also download a sample .docx to adapt offline.

Can I download the invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Fill it in online in your browser, then use Print → Save as PDF (or the built-in PDF button) to email a printable PDF invoice to your client.

Do the templates calculate VAT and totals for me?

Yes. Subtotals, VAT/GST (UK, EU, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, South Africa, Ireland), discounts and totals calculate automatically as you type line items. Your next invoice remembers your business details and number.

Is there a UK VAT invoice template?

Yes — pick the UK VAT starter from the By country list and it opens pre-set to 20% VAT with your VAT registration number field, matching HMRC rules for a full VAT invoice. Other starters handle Australian GST, Canadian GST/HST and more.

Is there a blank invoice template I can just fill in?

Yes. The Blank invoice starter is the simplest — open it, add line items, tax and your client details, then download the Word file or PDF.
