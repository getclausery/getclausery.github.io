Common questions

Are the invoice templates free?

Yes. Every invoice template on this page is free to fill in online and download as an editable .docx, with totals, tax and numbering worked out for you. No account or credit card required.

Do the invoices calculate totals automatically?

Yes. When you fill one in online, line totals, subtotal, tax, discount and grand total update as you type, and the next invoice number is suggested. Downloaded Word files keep the figures as plain numbers so you can edit them freely.

Can I add my logo and branding?

Yes. The app lets you upload a logo, set your brand colour and save your business details so future invoices pre-fill automatically on your device.
