Contract deadline calculator

Reviewed by Clausery editors · 24 October 2026

Work out notice periods, payment due dates, renewal dates and response deadlines. Month arithmetic stops at month end, so January 31 plus one month is the last day of February.

Things to check

  • Is the start day counted? This calculator counts from the day after the start date, which is the usual rule, but some contracts and courts say otherwise.
  • Weekends and holidays. Many rules move a deadline that falls on a non-business day to the next business day.
  • Time zones and cut-off times. "By 5pm" or "close of business" matters for filings and notices.

A calculation aid, not legal advice. Court and statutory deadlines follow their own rules.

Questions

How do you count days for a contract deadline?

The usual convention, used by this calculator, is to exclude the start day and count from the next day, so 10 days from 1 March is 11 March. Some contracts and court rules also move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday to the next business day; check the wording that applies to you.

What happens when adding months to the end of a month?

If the target month is shorter, the date moves to its last day, so one month after 31 January is 28 February (29 in a leap year).

What counts as a business day?

Monday to Friday, excluding any holidays you enter. Public holidays differ by country and state, so add the ones that apply to your contract.
