About Clausery

Clausery makes the documents people draft every week faster to produce, without asking them to upload anything. Your templates, answers and finished documents stay on your own computer.

Why it exists

Small businesses and freelancers repeatedly prepare quotes, invoices and receipts. Re-entering business details, checking totals and updating a Word document take attention away from the job itself. Clausery brings those steps together: answer the questions, check the amounts and download an editable document.

The app reads a Word template and builds the finished .docx on your device. Your templates, answers and documents are not uploaded to a document-processing server. Start with the free business document kit or choose a billing workflow for the task at hand.

What is free, and how Clausery makes money

  • The template library is free. All 87 Word templates, the clause library, the guides and the calculators are free, with no sign-up. Library templates never count towards any plan limit.
  • Automating your own templates is free for three. Upload up to three of your own Word templates and generate unlimited documents from them.
  • Pro pays for the work. Businesses that automate more of their own documents, or need calculations in those templates, encryption or client intake forms, buy a Pro or Team license.

There are no ads, no affiliate links and no tracking cookies. The app counts anonymous events, such as making a document from a library template, without recording document contents; this website uses cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics. Activating an online license sends the license key to Lemon Squeezy, separately from document processing. Read the privacy policy for the current data flows.

How it is built

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules, with document generation running locally in the browser.
  • Served as static files by GitHub Pages, and installable as an offline app.
  • The code is published on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does, and every release is listed in the changelog.
  • Optional encryption of everything stored in the browser (AES-256-GCM), and license checks that send only the key. See the security overview.

What Clausery is not

Clausery is software, not a law firm, and it does not give legal advice. The templates are general samples written in plain English; laws differ between countries and states, so have a document reviewed for your situation before you rely on it.
