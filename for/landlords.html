Free landlord and tenant letters, filled in online

Leases, move-in checklists, late rent notices, rent increases and deposit returns follow the same pattern every time, and most have a deadline. Questions

How much notice does a rent increase need?

Check the law for the property, tenancy type and lease before choosing a notice period. Fixed-term restrictions, rent controls, exemptions, required forms and the delivery method can change the answer. A deadline calculator counts dates; it does not determine which legal notice rule applies.

How long does a landlord have to return a security deposit?

Use the rule for the property and tenancy, not a deadline copied from another state. Check the event that starts the clock, itemised deduction requirements, any interest and the required delivery method. Preserve the inspection and payment records and obtain advice promptly if the deadline or a deduction is disputed.

Is a roommate agreement legally binding?

It can be enforced between the roommates like other agreements, but it does not change the lease. If you are all on the lease, the landlord can usually still claim the whole rent from any one of you.

How is this different from TurboTenant, Zillow or eForms?

Compare the service you need and the provider’s current terms: account-based property management, prescribed local forms and local Word drafting are different tasks. Clausery supplies general editable Word templates without a drafting account; it does not provide a hosted tenant portal, rent collection or a jurisdiction-specific legal-form service. Check local requirements and any required disclosures separately.

What does it cost?

The templates are free to download or fill in, with no limit. In the app you can also automate up to 3 of your own templates free; Pro is $19 per user per month.
