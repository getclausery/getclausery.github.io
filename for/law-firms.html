For law firms

Document automation for small law firms, without uploading client files

Engagement letters, NDAs, demand letters, wills and leases: most firms draft the same documents every week from Word files that already exist. Clausery turns those files into questionnaires and assembles the finished document on the lawyer's own computer.

Open Clausery, freeBrowse free templates

Confidentiality you can explain in one sentence

Document contents and answers are assembled locally rather than uploaded by Clausery. Your firm controls any exported files, recipients and record-storage process.

Keep your precedents

Your Word templates keep their styles, numbering and letterhead. Add tags where details change; nothing is re-created in a new editor.

Clients answer without a portal

Pro client intake creates a questionnaire as a single HTML file. The client fills it in offline and returns an answers file you review and import into a draft; there is no hosted inbox.

Encrypted on the device

Pro protects workspace templates and drafts with a passphrase and automatic locking. Downloaded backups and answers files need separate protection; workspace encryption does not encrypt those exports.

Questions

Is Clausery suitable for confidential client matters?

Documents and answers are assembled locally without uploading their contents to Clausery. Pro can encrypt workspace templates and drafts with a passphrase, but downloaded exports need separate protection. Review the setup, device security, sharing and retention against your own IT policies and professional rules.

Can we use our existing Word precedents?

Yes. Add {tags} where details change and wrap optional clauses in a section. Clausery fills the original Word file rather than rebuilding it in another editor. Review the generated document, especially numbering and page layout when sections or lists change.

How is it different from Gavel, Clio Draft or HotDocs?

Compare the complete workflow and selected deployment. Other tools may provide hosted interviews, integrations or central administration that local Word drafting does not replace. Features and plans vary; use the comparison pages to test the actual requirements rather than assuming every product has the same architecture.

What does it cost?

Every library template is free, and you can automate up to 3 of your own Word templates free, with unlimited documents. Pro, with unlimited templates, calculations, encryption and client intake forms, is $19 per user per month.
