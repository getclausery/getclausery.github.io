Who Clausery is for

Clausery suits anyone who sends the same kinds of documents again and again: invoices, contracts, HR letters and landlord forms. Each audience page shows the templates that team uses, the questions that change, and the guides that explain the wording.

Law firms

Engagement letters, NDAs, demand letters, wills and leases: most firms draft the same documents every week from Word files that already exist. Clausery turns those files into questionnaires and assembles the finished document on the lawyer's own computer..

Landlords and tenants

Leases, move-in checklists, late rent notices, rent increases and deposit returns follow the same pattern every time, and most have a deadline. Clausery fills in a free Word template from your answers and keeps tenant details on your computer.

17 templates · 14 related guides

What every team gets

  • The same approved wording. A template is set up once; everyone else answers plain-language questions and downloads the finished Word file.
  • Nothing uploaded. Names, salaries, client details and the document itself stay in the browser, which keeps vendor reviews and records of processing simpler.
  • Optional clauses that disappear when they do not apply. A bonus, probation period, guarantor or appeal right only appears when the answer calls for it.
  • Editable output. The result is an ordinary .docx that keeps the template's styles, numbering and letterhead, so it can still be edited or printed to PDF.
  • No per-document charge for library templates. Every template can be downloaded or filled in free, with no account and no document limit.

Not sure which page fits?
