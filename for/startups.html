Questions

What legal documents does a startup need first?

Usually an offer letter or employment agreement for the first hire, an independent-contractor agreement for freelancers, an NDA for early conversations, and invoices and quotes for getting paid. Clausery covers all of these free.

Does Clausery incorporate my company?

No. Incorporation and equity paperwork are handled by a formation service such as Clerky or Stripe Atlas. Use Clausery for the contracts, NDAs, offer letters and invoices you need once the company exists.

Is this legal advice?

No. Clausery is software that fills in templates. The templates are general samples; have a document reviewed for your jurisdiction before you rely on it.

What does it cost?

Every library template is free, with no account and no time limit. You can also automate up to 3 of your own templates free; Pro is $19 per user per month.
