Questions

Can I add a table of deliverables or fees?

Yes. Put the repeat tags in a table row and the row repeats for each deliverable or line item, keeping your borders and shading.

Can it calculate totals and dates?

Yes, on the Pro plan: computed fields can add up line items, work out a date 30 days after signing, or write an amount in words.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Once the app has loaded, it works offline, so you can draft on a train or at a client site.

What does it cost?

Free for up to three templates with unlimited documents. Pro is $19 per user per month.
