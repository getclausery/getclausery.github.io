How buying works

  1. Choose a plan and pay through our secure hosted checkout (Lemon Squeezy).
  2. Select Open Clausery after payment or in your receipt email. Your plan activates automatically and the app opens, ready to use. No separate account or key to copy.
  3. Start making documents. For another device, use the receipt button there. Renewals apply automatically; manage billing or cancel from app Settings. Keep the receipt access link private. For a change between Pro and Team or more seats, contact us.

Online checkout is not open yet, so keys are issued on request:

  1. Email getclausery@gmail.com. Say which plan you want and how many people will use it. No account is needed.
  2. We reply by email with how to pay and send the key.
  3. Paste the key in the app under Settings → License. An offline key is verified with a cryptographic signature entirely on this device.

Pricing questions

Is there a free trial of Pro?

Yes. Start it in the app with one click: every Pro feature for 14 days, with no card, no account and no email. The trial runs in your browser and simply ends. Nothing renews, and you keep everything you made. The Free plan itself has no time limit.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. If Clausery is not right for you, email getclausery@gmail.com within 30 days of your first payment for a plan and we will refund it in full. No forms, and you do not need to give a reason.

What is the “Made with Clausery” line?

On the Free plan, documents made in the app from library templates end with one small line, “Made with Clausery”, linked to this site. It is how a free product finds new users. Pro, Team and the free trial leave it out, and documents made from your own templates never have it.

What happens when a license expires?

The app falls back to the Free plan. Everything you created stays on your device and keeps working; only the Pro-gated features pause until you renew.

Do you offer discounts for legal aid, nonprofits or education?

Yes: 50% off Pro and Team. Contact getclausery@gmail.com with your organisation details before buying so we can arrange the discount.

Does the app contact a server to check my subscription?

The app activates your purchase automatically when you select Open Clausery after payment or in your receipt email. Behind the scenes, Lemon Squeezy supplies a license credential, which the app checks on activation and about once a week. It works offline for up to 30 days between checks. The credential and browser activation ID are sent, not your templates, answers or documents. Anonymous app usage counts are separate and contain nothing you type. Businesses that need no network can ask for an offline license.

Can I get an invoice or pay by bank transfer?

Team and Enterprise customers can pay by invoice. Ask for one when you request a key.

How do I activate my purchase?

After paying, select Open Clausery on the confirmation page or in your receipt email. Your plan activates automatically and opens the app, with no separate account and no key to copy. Use that receipt button on another device too. Keep the access link private.

How many devices can use a plan?

Pro is for one named user and allows activation in up to five browser profiles. Team covers five named users and allows up to fifteen browser profiles. In an unused browser, go to Settings → Advanced activation → Remove from this browser to free an activation, or contact support if you cannot access it.

How do I add Team seats?

The online Team checkout covers five named users at $49/month. Additional seats cost $9 per user/month and are arranged by request at getclausery@gmail.com. Contact us before purchasing for a larger team.
