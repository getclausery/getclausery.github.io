Plain-English answers to the questions people ask before they use a template, each linked to a free Word template you can fill in online.

Common questions

Are the guides free to read?

Yes. Every guide on this page is free to read, no sign-up or paywall. Each one links to the relevant free Word template so you can put the advice into practice directly.

Do the guides provide legal advice?

No. Guides explain how a document is usually structured and what the common options mean; they are drafting information, not legal advice tailored to your situation. Have a lawyer review important contracts.

How often are the guides updated?

Guides are reviewed by the Clausery editors (small-business owners, contract drafters and bookkeepers). The "Reviewed by" line at the top of each guide shows the last review date.
