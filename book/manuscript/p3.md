The audit has told us what to refuse. It has not yet told us what we have. This part builds the positive theology — not as a set of restrictions on the manifest space but as the thing the manifest space was reaching for and could not name. It begins with a word: *manifestation* is not a New Age term that the Church must tolerate. It is a biblical word, and in the Bible it is something God does. From there it develops the four scriptural pillars, the sacramental principle, the practice of manifestation of conscience, the engine room of providence, the anthropology of desire and *habitus*, and the discipline of hiddenness. By the end, the reader should be able to say not merely what is wrong with the law of attraction but what the truth is that the law of attraction is a parody of.
