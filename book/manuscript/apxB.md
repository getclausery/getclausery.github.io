title: The Triage Card
lede: One page. Photocopy it. It is not a substitute for a trained director, a confessor, or a clinician.
---

**Two questions, always first**

1. *"What do you think this practice is doing?"* — reveals the metaphysics.
2. *"If it stopped working tomorrow, what would you do?"* — reveals the god.

**Green — affirm and add the frame.**
Goal-setting · journalling · gratitude lists · positive self-talk about one's own capacities · habit, sleep and exercise routines from self-help · "manifesting" used loosely for hoping.

**Amber — catechesis and a substitute.**
Visualization of a desired good · affirmations · vision boards · strong-sense "manifesting" of a job or partner · crystals as aesthetics · astrology as entertainment · loose "energy" talk.

*Say:* "Almost everything you're doing is fine. The reason you were given for why it works is not, and here is a better one." *Give:* one practice from Part Four, written down, with a time.

**Red — renounce, and replace.**
Channeling or "higher self" guidance · mediums · attempts to control another's will · "reality shifting" · curses and hexes · telling a sick person she caused her illness · anything requiring secrecy from the Church, a spouse, or a director · any fee for spiritual power.

*Say it briefly, without disgust. Then put something in the space.*

**Six cases in one line each**

- **The teenager:** do not take the notebook; change the addressee. Give her a novena.
- **The scrupulous:** thoughts are not creative; temptation is not sin; the first movement is not consent. Repeat. Refer if severe.
- **The grieving:** "That is not true, and it is not what the Church teaches, and I am sorry you were told it." Then sit down.
- **The person in danger:** safety before metaphysics. Refer.
- **The person refusing treatment:** using medicine is not a lack of faith. "Take the medicine, and keep praying." Involve family and doctor the same day.
- **The person with an experience:** test the fruit; be slow; *nihil obstat* is not approval.

**The rule about the bedside**

No teaching, correction, or catechesis with the dying, the acutely grieving, or the newly diagnosed. Presence only. Say once, if it is true and the moment is right: *you did not do this, and you are not being punished, and God is not disgusted by you.*

**What to hand over**

- **One practice** — small, timed, written down.
- **One name** — a director, confessor, or chaplain, with a phone number.
- **One sentence** she will remember at 2 a.m.

**When to refer**

Persistent scrupulosity · intrusive thoughts causing distress · any suggestion of self-harm · suspected abuse · symptoms of psychosis · involvement with a group that demands secrecy or money. Refer to a clinician, a safeguarding officer, or both. This is not a failure of faith.
