A verdict is only as good as the test that produced it. This part builds four tests from the Thomistic tradition, applies them to the manifest space as a system, and then works through fourteen of its central claims one by one, assigning each a verdict: *integrate*, *correct*, or *reject*. It closes with a lexicon, because the argument will be won or lost at the level of vocabulary, and with a chapter on why argument alone cannot win it.
